A simple way to plan any trip

  1. Fix who, when and how much before choosing where. See how to plan a group trip.
  2. Draft the budget with real figures for your dates, and agree it.
  3. Book travel first, then stay, then the one or two things that need advance booking, on official sites only.
  4. Agree how costs are shared before anyone spends. See splitting costs fairly.
  5. Pack from a list, two or three days ahead.
  6. Run the going-away checklist on the day. See before you leave home.
  7. Record expenses daily in the splitter, and settle within a week of coming home.

Why there are no prices on this site

You will not find "a trip to this place costs so much" here. Such figures are out of date almost as soon as they are written, and they differ for every traveller. Instead, each tool takes your own numbers, and each guide says where to look them up: the operator's own website or counter. It is a little more work for you, and the answer is right.

Before you book anything

Use the official website or app of the railway, airline, bus corporation, hotel or temple trust, typed by hand or opened from a bookmark. Look-alike booking sites and "customer care" numbers found through search are a common way travellers lose money. Pay only to accounts in the business's own name, and be wary of any deal that needs the full amount at once to a personal account.

Is the cost splitter free?

Yes. There is no login, no limit on people or expenses, and no app to install.

Do I need the internet to use the tools?

Only to open the page the first time. The sums are done on your device.

Can you suggest how much my trip will cost?

No. Costs depend on your dates, choices and destination. The budget planner adds up the figures you find and enter.
