Visa on arrival for Indian passport holders

These countries and regions issue a visa on arrival to Indian ordinary passport holders, according to the Ministry of External Affairs (page updated 27 August 2026). On-arrival does not always mean "turn up with nothing": some countries ask for papers in advance, or only offer it to people who hold a visa or residence permit of another country.
