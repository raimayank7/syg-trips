The country pages repeat what the Ministry of External Affairs lists. These guides explain what the words mean: visa free, visa on arrival, e-visa; how to avoid look-alike websites; and what border officers commonly ask for. They do not replace the official sites, and they state no visa fees or processing times, because those change.

What is inside

Visa free, visa on arrival and e-visa explained. The three words sound similar but mean three different journeys. Know which one you are on before you book. Sections: Why there are three lists; Visa free; Visa on arrival; E-visa; Which is best?.

How to avoid fake and look-alike visa websites. Many people pay extra, or lose their money, on websites that only look official. A few checks keep you safe. Sections: The problem; Five checks before you pay; What you should never share; If you already paid the wrong site; Keep proof.

What border officers commonly ask for. Immigration is usually quick if your papers are in order and you can answer a few simple questions. A little preparation makes it so. Sections: The basics; Documents commonly asked for; Questions you may be asked; Pack a small folder; Know your dates; If you are refused.

Passport validity and blank pages: what to check. Two small details of your passport cause more cancelled trips than anything else. Check them before you pay for a ticket. Sections: Why validity matters; How to check your passport; Blank pages; Passport condition; When to renew; Names must match; E-visa and passport number.

When the lists disagree: what to do. You may find one source saying visa free and another saying e-visa for the same country. Here is how to decide. Sections: Why sources differ; The order of trust; What to do when you find a conflict; Re-check before you fly; This site's approach.

Check before you travel. This page repeats what the Ministry of External Affairs (India) lists for Indian ordinary passport holders; its page was last updated on 27 August 2026 and we downloaded it on 14 November 2026. Rules change without notice and a country can differ from the MEA list. Confirm on the destination country's own immigration or embassy website and on the MEA page before you book.
