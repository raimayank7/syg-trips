Passport validity and blank pages: what to check

Two small details of your passport cause more cancelled trips than anything else. Check them before you pay for a ticket.

Why validity matters

A visa or visa-free stay lets you stay for a period, but the country also wants to be sure that your passport will still be valid for the whole of that period, and often some time afterwards, in case of a delay or an emergency. That is why many countries ask that your passport be valid for a minimum number of months beyond the date you enter or leave. Six months is a common figure, but some countries ask for more or less, and some only need validity for the length of the stay. The MEA remark for a country sometimes states the requirement; where it does not, check the destination's immigration site.

Airlines also check. An airline can refuse to let you board if your passport does not meet the destination's rule, because the airline may have to bring you back and pay a fine. This is why a validity problem usually shows up at the check-in desk, not at the border.

How to check your passport

Look at the "Date of Expiry" on the photo page. Add the number of months the destination asks for to the date you will enter, and see whether the expiry is later than that date. For example, if you plan to enter on the first of March and the country asks for six months of validity, your passport must be valid until at least the first of September.

Count the date of your return, not only your arrival, and add a margin for a delay or a longer stay.

Blank pages

Some countries ask for a minimum number of blank pages, usually so that there is space for entry and exit stamps and a visa. Pages that are only "Endorsement" pages at the back may not count. If your passport has few blank pages left, consider replacing it before a long trip.

Passport condition

A passport that is damaged, water-stained, torn, or has pages that have come loose can be refused. If yours is worn, replace it. Avoid writing in it, and keep it in a cover.

When to renew

Renew early. Passport processing takes time, and there may be a wait for an appointment. Do not book non-refundable tickets until you have checked that your passport meets the destination's rule, or until your renewed passport is in hand.

Names must match

The name on your ticket, your visa and your passport must match exactly. A small difference, such as a missing middle name or a different spelling, may cause delay or refusal. Check each document when you receive it, and correct a mistake at once.

E-visa and passport number

An e-visa is linked to the passport number you entered when you applied. If you get a new passport after applying, the e-visa will not match and you will need to apply again. Use the passport that you will travel with.

Can I travel on a passport that expires during my stay?

Often not. Many countries do not allow it. Check the destination's rule, and renew before travelling if in doubt.

Does validity count from the day I arrive or the day I leave?

It depends on the country. Use the stricter reading, from the day you leave, as your planning rule.
