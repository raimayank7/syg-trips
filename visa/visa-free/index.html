Visa-free countries for Indian passport holders

The Ministry of External Affairs lists these countries and regions as allowing Indian ordinary passport holders to enter without a visa (page updated 27 August 2026). The period shows how long you may stay. Many entries have conditions such as holding a visa or residence permit of certain other countries, a return ticket, or registration on an arrival website, so open a country for the full MEA remark.
