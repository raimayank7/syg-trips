Visa for Indians: Djibouti

The Ministry of External Affairs lists Djibouti under visa on arrival and e-visa for Indian ordinary passport holders (page updated 27 August 2026).

Visa on arrival for Indian passport holders

You do not apply before you travel; the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival. Fees, queues and conditions apply, and some countries want papers sent in advance.

The MEA lists no further conditions for this facility.

E-visa for Indian passport holders

You must apply online before you travel, on the government's own e-visa website, and carry the approval with you. Apply only on the official site: look-alike websites charge extra.

The MEA lists no further conditions for this facility.

The MEA lists Djibouti in more than one table (visa on arrival and e-visa). That usually means different routes or conditions: for example, one for people who hold a visa or residence permit from certain other countries, and another for everyone else. Read each remark carefully, and confirm with the embassy which one applies to you.

What to prepare

For most trips under this facility you should have a passport valid for the period the country asks (often six months), passport photos, the visa fee in the currency the airport accepts (check which), a return ticket and proof of accommodation.

Before you go: plugs and weather

  • Plugs: Djibouti uses type C/E sockets at 220 V (IEC list, archived June 2024).
  • Weather in Djibouti: the most comfortable months for sightseeing are November to March (NASA POWER averages, 2001 to 2025).

Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Djibouti?

The MEA lists Djibouti under visa on arrival and e-visa for Indian ordinary passport holders. You do not apply before you travel; the visa is issued at the border or airport on arrival. Fees, queues and conditions apply, and some countries want papers sent in advance. Confirm on the official site before you book.

What should I carry when travelling to Djibouti?

Usually: a passport valid for the period the country asks (often six months), passport photos, the visa fee in the currency the airport accepts (check which), a return ticket and proof of accommodation. The exact list is set by Djibouti and can change: check its immigration website or the embassy.

Where can I check the latest rule for Djibouti?

On the website of Djibouti's immigration service or its embassy or high commission in India, and on the MEA page "Visa Facility for Indian Nationals". Do not rely on third-party blogs for the final answer.
