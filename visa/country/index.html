These 108 countries and regions appear in at least one of the Ministry of External Affairs' three lists for Indian ordinary passport holders: visa free, visa on arrival and e-visa. A country that is not here may still let you in with a normal visa applied for at its embassy; this site covers only the facilities the MEA lists. Rules change, so always confirm with the destination.

Check before you travel. This page repeats what the Ministry of External Affairs (India) lists for Indian ordinary passport holders; its page was last updated on 27 August 2026 and we downloaded it on 14 November 2026. Rules change without notice and a country can differ from the MEA list. Confirm on the destination country's own immigration or embassy website and on the MEA page before you book.
