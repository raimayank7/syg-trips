    How to use this site

    Start with the country you plan to visit. The page tells you which of the three facilities the Ministry of External Affairs lists for Indian ordinary passport holders, quotes the ministry's remarks, and explains what to prepare. If the country is not listed at all, you normally need a visa from its embassy or visa centre in advance, which this site does not cover.

    The data are the ministry's, copied on 14 November 2026 from the page it last updated on 27 August 2026. Governments can change entry rules at short notice and the MEA's separate pages do not always agree, so treat this as a first check and finish with the destination country's own official website.

    Check before you travel. This page repeats what the Ministry of External Affairs (India) lists for Indian ordinary passport holders; its page was last updated on 27 August 2026 and we downloaded it on 14 November 2026. Rules change without notice and a country can differ from the MEA list. Confirm on the destination country's own immigration or embassy website and on the MEA page before you book.
