Plug type D: shape, countries and voltage

A type D plug has three round pins in a triangle. It is the older large three-pin plug used in India and some neighbouring countries. 35 countries and territories in the IEC list use it, among them Bangladesh, Bhutan, Botswana, Chad, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Dominica, French Guiana, Ghana, Guadeloupe, Guyana, Hong Kong, India.

Which plugs fit

Indian three-pin plugs of this kind fit the matching sockets in India and a few neighbouring countries. Types D and M look alike but differ in pin size and the current they carry, so do not force one into the other's socket.

Voltage with this plug

Countries using type D run on 127 V, 220 V, 230 V and 240 V. A plug shape tells you nothing about the voltage, so always check the supply as well as the socket.

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none.
