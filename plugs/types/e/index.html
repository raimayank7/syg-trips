Plug type E: shape, countries and voltage

A type E plug has two round pins with a hole for the socket's earth pin. It is the French and Belgian style: the earth pin sits in the socket, not on the plug. 36 countries and territories in the IEC list use it, among them Belgium, Benin, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad, Comoros, Czechia, Côte d'Ivoire, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Denmark.

Which plugs fit

A type C two-pin plug fits this socket. A plug of this type does not fit sockets of other types without an adapter, and the type E and F plugs are not interchangeable in every case: a type E plug has a hole for the socket's earth pin, which a type F socket does not provide.

Voltage with this plug

Countries using type E run on 120 V, 127 V, 220 V and 230 V. A plug shape tells you nothing about the voltage, so always check the supply as well as the socket.
