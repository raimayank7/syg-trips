Plug type M: shape, countries and voltage

A type M plug has three large round pins in a triangle. It is the large South African plug, also found in India and its neighbours for heavier appliances. 17 countries and territories in the IEC list use it, among them Bhutan, Botswana, Eswatini, India, Kuwait, Lesotho, Macao, Malaysia, Mozambique, Namibia, Nepal, Pakistan.

Which plugs fit

Indian three-pin plugs of this kind fit the matching sockets in India and a few neighbouring countries. Types D and M look alike but differ in pin size and the current they carry, so do not force one into the other's socket.

Voltage with this plug

Countries using type M run on 220 V, 230 V and 240 V. A plug shape tells you nothing about the voltage, so always check the supply as well as the socket.

Countries that list type M

  • Bhutan 230 V, C/D/F/G/M
  • Botswana 230 V, D/G/M
  • Eswatini 230 V, M
  • India 230 V, C/D/M
  • Kuwait 240 V, C/G/M
  • Lesotho 220 V, M
  • Macao 220 V, D/F/G/M
  • Malaysia 240 V, A/C/G/M
  • Mozambique 220 V, C/F/M
  • Namibia 220 V, D/M
  • Nepal 230 V, C/D/M
  • Pakistan 230 V, C/D/G/M
  • Singapore 230 V, C/G/M
  • South Africa 230 V, C/M/N
  • Sri Lanka 230 V, D/G/M
  • United Arab Emirates 230 V, G/M
  • Yemen 240 V, D/G/M

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none. Check your device label and ask your hotel or airline for anything that matters, such as medical equipment.
