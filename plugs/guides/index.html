Plug Guide

Guides

How to charge and power your things safely abroad.

  • Travel adapter or voltage converter: which do you need?
  • How to read the power label on your devices
  • Hair dryers, kettles and irons abroad
  • Charging phones and laptops abroad

These guides explain the difference between an adapter and a converter, how to read the label on a charger, what to do about hair dryers and other high-power appliances, and what to pack for a trip. They are written for ordinary travellers and avoid jargon. For a quick answer for a country, start from the country list or the adapter finder.

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none. Check your device label and ask your hotel or airline for anything that matters, such as medical equipment.
