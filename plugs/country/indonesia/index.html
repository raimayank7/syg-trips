Plug type and voltage in Indonesia

Indonesia uses type C and type F sockets at 220 V or 110 V and 50 Hz, according to the IEC World Plugs list (archived June 2024). Below you will find what each plug looks like, whether your Indian plugs and appliances will work, and what to pack.

C, F
Voltage

50 Hz
India for comparison
230 V, 50 Hz, types C, D and M

What the plugs look like

  • Type C: two round pins. This is the small two-pin plug that most phone chargers, shavers and slim laptop adapters use around the world; it is often called the Europlug.
  • Type F: two round pins with earth clips on the sides. This is the German style, often called Schuko, with metal clips at the top and bottom of the plug for the earth connection.

From India: will your plugs fit?

The small two-pin plug (type C) that most Indian phone and laptop chargers use fits type C and type F sockets, so those chargers should plug straight in. Larger three-pin Indian plugs (types D and M), such as on an iron or kettle, will not fit: you need an adapter for them.

Voltage and frequency

The supply here (220 V or 110 V) is in the same range as India's 230 V, so Indian appliances work at the right voltage.

The frequency is 50 Hz, the same as India, so clocks and motors behave as at home.

What to pack

A universal travel adapter covers every type in this list. Choose one with built-in surge protection if you carry a laptop, and one with USB ports if you want to charge a phone without a second plug. Carry one adapter per device you charge at the same time, or a multi-way strip that you plug into a single adapter. Check the maximum power rating of the adapter against what you plug in: a hair dryer or kettle draws far more than a phone.

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none. Check your device label and ask your hotel or airline for anything that matters, such as medical equipment.

Questions

Do I need an adapter for Indonesia?

The small two-pin plug (type C) that most Indian phone and laptop chargers use fits type C and type F sockets, so those chargers should plug straight in. Larger three-pin Indian plugs (types D and M), such as on an iron or kettle, will not fit: you need an adapter for them.

Will my Indian phone charger work in Indonesia?

Most modern phone and laptop chargers are marked "100-240 V, 50-60 Hz", which means they accept the supply anywhere in the world; only the plug shape differs. Read the small print on your own charger to be sure. Here the plug part is the only thing to solve.

What is the voltage in Indonesia?

220 V or 110 V at 50 Hz.
