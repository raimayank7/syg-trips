Countries with 115 V electricity

4 countries and territories in the IEC list give 115 V as a household supply voltage. This puts them in the 100 to 127 V group, like the United States and Japan.

An appliance marked for 230 V only will not work properly on 115 V, and may be damaged. Phone and laptop chargers marked 100-240 V are safe. For hair dryers, irons and kettles, use a converter rated for the appliance's power, or buy a local one.

Countries

  • Barbados 115 V, A/B
  • Bolivia 115 V or 230 V, A/C
  • El Salvador 115 V, A/B
  • Trinidad and Tobago 115 V, A/B

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none. Check your device label and ask your hotel or airline for anything that matters, such as medical equipment.
