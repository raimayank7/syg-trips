Voltage and frequency

Which countries run on which supply.

By voltage

  • 100 V 1 countries
  • 110 V 15 countries
  • 115 V 4 countries
  • 120 V 17 countries
  • 127 V 8 countries
  • 220 V 74 countries
  • 230 V 88 countries
  • 240 V 21 countries

By frequency

  • 50 Hz 174 countries
  • 60 Hz 45 countries

The world is split into two main groups. Around 220 to 240 volts at 50 hertz is the standard in India, Europe, most of Asia, Africa and Australia. Around 100 to 127 volts at 60 hertz is the standard in North America, parts of Central and South America, Taiwan and, with a twist, Japan, which uses 100 volts and has both 50 and 60 hertz in different parts of the country. Pick a value to see which countries use it. Voltage matters more to your devices than the plug shape, because a wrong voltage can damage an appliance, while a wrong plug just does not fit.

Check before you travel. Plug, voltage and frequency data come from the IEC World Plugs list as archived on 2 June 2024. Standards and wiring change, and hotels often have extra sockets or none. Check your device label and ask your hotel or airline for anything that matters, such as medical equipment.
