    What jet lag is, in short

    Jet lag is the tiredness, poor sleep and fuzzy thinking that follow a quick trip across time zones, because the body's internal clock is still on home time. It passes as the clock adjusts, usually within days. This site works out the time difference for your trip and turns the official advice into a plan: when to shift your sleep before you fly, what to do on the flight, and how to use daylight after you land.

    The advice comes from the CDC's Yellow Book and the NHS and is general wellness information. We do not give medical advice and we do not suggest medicines or doses.
