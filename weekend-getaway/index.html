Weekend trip packing list

For two nights you need far less than you think. One small backpack or cabin bag is enough, and travelling light means no waiting for luggage, easy changes of plan and nothing to guard. The trick is to choose clothes that all go with each other.

Tips for this kind of trip

  • Lay out what you plan to take, then put back one top and one "just in case" item.
  • Stick to two or three colours so that every top goes with every bottom.
  • Roll clothes instead of folding them: they crease less and fit better in a small bag.
  • Before leaving home: gas off, taps closed, windows locked, fridge cleared of what will spoil, plants watered, and geyser and iron unplugged.
  • Book the return journey before you leave. Sunday evening trains, buses and roads are the busiest of the week.
  • Check opening days of the places you want to see; many are closed one day a week.
  • Leave on Friday evening or very early on Saturday to gain most of a day.

Questions

What size bag do I need for a weekend trip?

A backpack or cabin bag of about 20 to 30 litres holds two or three days of clothes and essentials for one person.

What do people forget most on short trips?

Phone chargers, medicines, toothbrushes, a light layer for cold transport, and their identity card.
