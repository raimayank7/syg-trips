Trips

About Trips

Trips is a free set of tools and checklists for planning a journey with family or friends: packing lists for different kinds of trip, a packing list maker, a cost splitter that works out who owes whom, a trip budget planner, a fuel cost calculator, and plain guides to planning and travelling well.

No prices are printed here

Fares, room rates, ticket prices, fuel prices, tolls, baggage limits, visa fees and entry charges change constantly and differ by place, date and operator. We do not print any of them. The calculators work on the figures you enter, and the guides tell you which official source to take each figure from: the railway, airline, hotel, highway authority, embassy or trust concerned.

Where the content comes from

  • Every list and guide is our own writing, from ordinary travel experience. Nothing is copied from other sites or guidebooks.
  • The splitter's arithmetic is checked by an automated test of known cases, including that shares always add up exactly to the amount spent.
  • Rules of airlines, railways, shrines, countries and authorities are deliberately described only in general terms, with a pointer to the official source, because they change. Written in November 2026.
  • Health notes, such as altitude and travel with children or elders, are general cautions, not medical advice. Ask your doctor about your own case.

Your trip stays on your device

The tools run in your browser. Names, expenses, budgets and ticked items are stored only in your own browser so that you can return to them; they are not sent to us or to anyone. Clearing your browser data removes them, so share or print a summary you want to keep.

What this site is not

It is not a travel agent or a booking site. It sells no tickets, tours or insurance, takes no payments, and is not connected with any airline, railway, hotel, app or authority named on it.

Who makes it

This site is part of ShareYourGroup, a family of free sites about the things people share, send and do together. It is free to use and needs no login. Advertisements pay for the hosting and the work.
