Packing list for travelling with a baby or children

Children travel well when they are fed, rested and occupied, and badly when any one of those fails. Pack the day bag as if the main luggage might not be reachable for twelve hours: food, nappies, a change of clothes and comfort items for that long.

Tips for this kind of trip

  • Ask your paediatrician before a long trip, a hill journey or a first flight with a baby, especially about medicines and ear discomfort.
  • Feeding a baby or giving a toddler something to sip during take-off and landing helps the ears.
  • Keep the child's routine for meals and sleep as far as you can, and plan journeys around nap time.
  • In a car, a child rides in the rear seat in a child seat suited to age and size, never on a lap in front.
  • Dress children in bright colours in crowded places, and teach older children to stand still and ask a shopkeeper or a mother with children for help if lost.
  • Use only sealed or boiled water for a baby's feeds.
  • Plan less. One outing and one rest a day keeps everyone cheerful.

Questions

How many nappies should I pack?

Count one for every two to three hours of the journey, add a day's extra, and buy the rest at your destination if it is a town.

How do I keep a toddler busy on a long journey?

Carry several small activities and bring them out one at a time: stickers, crayons, a few toys, snacks in small boxes, and a downloaded show as the last resort.
