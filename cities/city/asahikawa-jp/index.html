*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Asahikawa has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 4.9 degrees C and about 1,045 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 23.8 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -12.6 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 28.9 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is spread through the year. August is the wettest month with about 147 mm and April the driest with about 49 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to April, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 32.7 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about -25.1 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 409 metres, which is 288 metres higher than the city (about 121 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 1.9 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Asahikawa

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Asahikawa are June. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 27 minutes, and around 21 December for about 8 hours 55 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 43.8 degrees).

How much rain does Asahikawa get?

About 1,045 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 147 mm) and April the driest (about 49 mm).

Is Asahikawa cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -12.6 degrees C and the average daytime high about -6.1 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in November to April.

What time zone is Asahikawa in?

Asahikawa uses the Asia/Tokyo time zone, which is UTC+9:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
