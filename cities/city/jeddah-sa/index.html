Weather and best time to visit Jeddah

*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Jeddah has a hot, very dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 29.9 degrees C and about 38 mm of rain falls over the year. June is the hottest month, with days reaching about 40.8 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 18.3 degrees C. Rain is rare at any time of year; even the wettest month, November, brings only about 12 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in April to October, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 49.1 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 12.1 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Jeddah

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Jeddah are December to February. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April to October. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 27 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 49 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 21.5 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

How much rain does Jeddah get?

About 38 mm a year in this data. November is the wettest month (about 12 mm) and July the driest (about 0 mm).

How hot does Jeddah get?

The hottest month is June, when the average daily high is about 40.8 degrees C and the average night-time low about 27.3 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Jeddah in?

Jeddah uses the Asia/Riyadh time zone, which is UTC+3:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
