*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Saransk has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 4.1 degrees C and about 613 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 25.2 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -15.5 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 32.2 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is spread through the year. June is the wettest month with about 73 mm and February the driest with about 36 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to April, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 40.8 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -40.0 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Saransk

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Saransk are May to August. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to April. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 17 hours 11 minutes, and around 21 December for about 7 hours 20 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 54.2 degrees).

How much rain does Saransk get?

About 613 mm a year in this data. June is the wettest month (about 73 mm) and February the driest (about 36 mm).

Is Saransk cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -15.5 degrees C and the average daytime high about -8.9 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in November to April.

What time zone is Saransk in?

Saransk uses the Europe/Moscow time zone, which is UTC+3:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
