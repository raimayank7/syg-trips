*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Haikou has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 24.7 degrees C and about 1,841 mm of rain falls over the year. June is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.3 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 16.6 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to October bring about 61% of the year's rain, while February is the driest month with about 28 mm. April to October feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 36.2 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about 9.0 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Haikou

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Haikou are November to December. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to October. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 21 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 55 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 20.0 degrees).

How much rain does Haikou get?

About 1,841 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 313 mm) and February the driest (about 28 mm).

How hot does Haikou get?

The hottest month is June, when the average daily high is about 30.3 degrees C and the average night-time low about 26.5 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Haikou in?

Haikou uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
