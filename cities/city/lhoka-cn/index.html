*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Lhoka has a cold, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 1.5 degrees C and about 418 mm of rain falls over the year. June is the hottest month, with days reaching about 16.8 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -15.9 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: June to August bring about 74% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 1 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in September to May, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 23.3 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about -22.4 degrees C, in December. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 4,665 metres, which is 1,105 metres higher than the city (about 3,560 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 7.2 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Lhoka

No month in Lhoka is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, May, June and September come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for September to May; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 01 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 16 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 29.2 degrees).

Questions about Lhoka

When is the best time to visit Lhoka?

No month in Lhoka is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, May, June and September come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

How much rain does Lhoka get?

About 418 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 135 mm) and January the driest (about 1 mm).

Is Lhoka cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -15.9 degrees C and the average daytime high about -0.9 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in September to May.

What time zone is Lhoka in?

Lhoka uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
