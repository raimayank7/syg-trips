*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Quanzhou has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 20.7 degrees C and about 1,532 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.5 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 8.0 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. June is the wettest month with about 244 mm and October the driest with about 44 mm. June to September feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 37.9 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -1.0 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Quanzhou

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Quanzhou are October to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in December to March; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for May to June and August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 41 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 35 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 24.9 degrees).

How much rain does Quanzhou get?

How hot does Quanzhou get?

The hottest month is July, when the average daily high is about 30.5 degrees C and the average night-time low about 25.1 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

Quanzhou uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
