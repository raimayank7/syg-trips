High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Roorkee has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 23.2 degrees C and about 1,290 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 39.1 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 7.1 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to September bring about 71% of the year's rain, while November is the driest month with about 5 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in April to June, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon. August feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 46.4 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 1.8 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 565 metres, which is 297 metres higher than the city (about 268 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 1.9 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Roorkee

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Roorkee are October to March. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in December to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to September; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April to June. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 04 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 13 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 29.9 degrees).

Questions about Roorkee

When is the best time to visit Roorkee?

How much rain does Roorkee get?

About 1,290 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 381 mm) and November the driest (about 5 mm).

Is Roorkee cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 7.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 18.8 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Roorkee in?

Roorkee uses the Asia/Kolkata time zone, which is UTC+5:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
