Weather and best time to visit Bern

Bern is a city in Switzerland (Europe) with a population of about 121,631 and is the national capital. It lies at 46.95° north, 7.45° east, about 549 metres above sea level. The numbers below show its typical weather month by month, the months that suit sightseeing best, what to pack and how long the days are.

*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Bern has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 8.5 degrees C and about 1,142 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 22.7 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -4.0 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. May is the wettest month with about 130 mm and February the driest with about 62 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to March, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.1 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -19.7 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Bern

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Bern are June and September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to March. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 53 minutes, and around 21 December for about 8 hours 31 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 46.9 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

How much rain does Bern get?

About 1,142 mm a year in this data. May is the wettest month (about 130 mm) and February the driest (about 62 mm).

Is Bern cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -4.0 degrees C and the average daytime high about 2.4 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to March.

What time zone is Bern in?

Bern uses the Europe/Zurich time zone, which is UTC+1:00 in January and UTC+2:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
