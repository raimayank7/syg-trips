High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Kuecuekcekmece has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 15.2 degrees C and about 624 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 29.0 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 3.5 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. December is the wettest month with about 82 mm and July the driest with about 20 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 38.1 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -5.9 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Kuecuekcekmece

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Kuecuekcekmece are May to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for January to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for December to January. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 08 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 13 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 41.0 degrees).
