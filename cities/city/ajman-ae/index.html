*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Ajman has a hot, very dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 28.6 degrees C and about 85 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 40.2 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 17.1 degrees C. Rain is rare at any time of year; even the wettest month, January, brings only about 23 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in May to September, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 46.1 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 10.9 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Ajman

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Ajman are November to March. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for May to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 43 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 33 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 25.4 degrees).

How much rain does Ajman get?

About 85 mm a year in this data. January is the wettest month (about 23 mm) and May the driest (about 1 mm).

How hot does Ajman get?

The hottest month is July, when the average daily high is about 40.2 degrees C and the average night-time low about 31.2 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Ajman in?

Ajman uses the Asia/Dubai time zone, which is UTC+4:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
