High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Aihara has a mild, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 13.9 degrees C and about 1,638 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 29.9 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -2.4 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. October is the wettest month with about 222 mm and January the driest with about 66 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to February, so expect frost and, at times, snow. July to August feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 37.5 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -7.0 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Aihara

No month in Aihara is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, April, May and June come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for September to October. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 34 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 45 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 35.6 degrees).

How much rain does Aihara get?

About 1,638 mm a year in this data. October is the wettest month (about 222 mm) and January the driest (about 66 mm).

Is Aihara cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -2.4 degrees C and the average daytime high about 6.9 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to February.

What time zone is Aihara in?

Aihara uses the Asia/Tokyo time zone, which is UTC+9:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
