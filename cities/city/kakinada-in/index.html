*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Kakinada has a hot, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 27.6 degrees C and about 1,228 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 33.4 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 21.8 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: June to October bring about 82% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 5 mm. July to September feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 41.7 degrees C, in May, and the coldest about 19.3 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Kakinada

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Kakinada are November to February. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to October; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April to May. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 08 minutes, and around 21 December for about 11 hours 07 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 17.0 degrees).

Questions about Kakinada

When is the best time to visit Kakinada?

How much rain does Kakinada get?

About 1,228 mm a year in this data. October is the wettest month (about 215 mm) and January the driest (about 5 mm).

How hot does Kakinada get?

The hottest month is May, when the average daily high is about 33.4 degrees C and the average night-time low about 28.2 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Kakinada in?

Kakinada uses the Asia/Kolkata time zone, which is UTC+5:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
