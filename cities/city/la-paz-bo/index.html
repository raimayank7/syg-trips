*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

La Paz has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 7.6 degrees C and about 647 mm of rain falls over the year. November is the hottest month, with days reaching about 15.8 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near -2.5 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 4.3 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is concentrated: December to February bring about 55% of the year's rain, while June is the driest month with about 9 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in May to August, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 22.7 degrees C, in November, and the coldest about -7.3 degrees C, in June. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit La Paz

No month in La Paz is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, September, October and November come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for May to August; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for December to February. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 11 hours 09 minutes, and around 21 December for about 13 hours 07 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -16.5 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

Questions about La Paz

When is the best time to visit La Paz?

No month in La Paz is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, September, October and November come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

How much rain does La Paz get?

About 647 mm a year in this data. January is the wettest month (about 136 mm) and June the driest (about 9 mm).

Is La Paz cold in winter?

In July the average night-time low is about -2.5 degrees C and the average daytime high about 12.8 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in May to August.

What time zone is La Paz in?

La Paz uses the America/La Paz time zone, which is UTC-4:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
