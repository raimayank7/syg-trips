*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Hafizabad has a hot, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 25.5 degrees C and about 617 mm of rain falls over the year. June is the hottest month, with days reaching about 43.3 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 5.3 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. July is the wettest month with about 178 mm and November the driest with about 8 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in April to September, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 50.6 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about -0.9 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Hafizabad

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Hafizabad are November to March. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in December to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to August; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 15 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 03 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 32.1 degrees).

How much rain does Hafizabad get?

About 617 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 178 mm) and November the driest (about 8 mm).

Is Hafizabad cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 5.3 degrees C and the average daytime high about 19.5 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Hafizabad in?

Hafizabad uses the Asia/Karachi time zone, which is UTC+5:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
