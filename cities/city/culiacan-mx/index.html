High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Culiacan has a hot, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 25.3 degrees C and about 705 mm of rain falls over the year. June is the hottest month, with days reaching about 37.0 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 13.4 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to September bring about 79% of the year's rain, while May is the driest month with about 1 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in May to June, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon. September feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 45.9 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 3.7 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Culiacan

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Culiacan are November to February. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to September; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for May to June. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 41 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 36 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 24.8 degrees).

How much rain does Culiacan get?

About 705 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 204 mm) and May the driest (about 1 mm).

How hot does Culiacan get?

The hottest month is June, when the average daily high is about 37.0 degrees C and the average night-time low about 23.6 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Culiacan in?

Culiacan uses the America/Mazatlan time zone, which is UTC-7:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
