High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Sariwon-si has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 10.4 degrees C and about 964 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 28.8 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -9.9 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 29.7 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is spread through the year. July is the wettest month with about 265 mm and January the driest with about 10 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to March, so expect frost and, at times, snow. July to August feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 37.5 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -23.9 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Sariwon-si

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Sariwon-si are May to June and September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to March; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 51 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 28 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 38.5 degrees).
