High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Avellaneda has a warm, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 18.0 degrees C and about 1,103 mm of rain falls over the year. January is the hottest month, with days reaching about 27.8 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 8.8 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. March is the wettest month with about 123 mm and June the driest with about 54 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.7 degrees C, in January, and the coldest about 3.1 degrees C, in July. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Avellaneda

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Avellaneda are October to January and April. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in June to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 9 hours 50 minutes, and around 21 December for about 14 hours 29 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -34.7 degrees).
