High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Tarlac City has a hot, very wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 26.7 degrees C and about 2,753 mm of rain falls over the year. April is the hottest month, with days reaching about 35.9 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 19.5 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 5.1 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is concentrated: June to September bring about 71% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 25 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in April, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon. June to December feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 41.8 degrees C, in April, and the coldest about 14.9 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Tarlac City

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Tarlac City are January. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to September; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 03 minutes, and around 21 December for about 11 hours 12 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 15.5 degrees).

How much rain does Tarlac City get?

How hot does Tarlac City get?

The hottest month is April, when the average daily high is about 35.9 degrees C and the average night-time low about 23.3 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Tarlac City in?

Tarlac City uses the Asia/Manila time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
