Weather and best time to visit Basse-Terre

High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Basse-Terre has a hot, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 27.2 degrees C and about 1,170 mm of rain falls over the year. September is the hottest month, with days reaching about 29.1 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near 25.0 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 2.6 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. October is the wettest month with about 172 mm and February the driest with about 31 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 30.6 degrees C, in September, and the coldest about 23.4 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Basse-Terre

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Basse-Terre are December to June. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for September to October. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 05 minutes, and around 21 December for about 11 hours 10 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 16.0 degrees).

How much rain does Basse-Terre get?

How hot does Basse-Terre get?

The hottest month is September, when the average daily high is about 29.1 degrees C and the average night-time low about 27.8 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

Basse-Terre uses the America/Guadeloupe time zone, which is UTC-4:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
