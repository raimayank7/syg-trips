*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Varna has a mild, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 13.8 degrees C and about 538 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 27.1 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 2.0 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. October is the wettest month with about 60 mm and April the driest with about 30 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 33.5 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -12.4 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Varna

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Varna are May to September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for December to March. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 23 minutes, and around 21 December for about 8 hours 59 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 43.2 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

How much rain does Varna get?

About 538 mm a year in this data. October is the wettest month (about 60 mm) and April the driest (about 30 mm).

Is Varna cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 2.0 degrees C and the average daytime high about 5.8 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Varna in?

Varna uses the Europe/Sofia time zone, which is UTC+2:00 in January and UTC+3:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
