*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Xining has a cool, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 5.2 degrees C and about 504 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 23.5 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -15.0 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: June to September bring about 73% of the year's rain, while December is the driest month with about 2 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in October to April, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.5 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -22.0 degrees C, in December. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 2,674 metres, which is 399 metres higher than the city (about 2,275 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 2.6 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Xining

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Xining are June to August. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for October to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 40 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 39 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 36.6 degrees).

How much rain does Xining get?

About 504 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 108 mm) and December the driest (about 2 mm).

Is Xining cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -15.0 degrees C and the average daytime high about -0.4 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in October to April.

What time zone is Xining in?

Xining uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
