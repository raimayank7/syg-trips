*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Aurora has a cool, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 9.0 degrees C and about 440 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.1 degrees C on average, and December is the coolest, with nights near -7.8 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. July is the wettest month with about 62 mm and January the driest with about 17 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to April, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 37.1 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -25.2 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 2,095 metres, which is 444 metres higher than the city (about 1,651 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 2.9 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Aurora

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Aurora are June to September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 59 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 21 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 39.7 degrees).

How much rain does Aurora get?

About 440 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 62 mm) and January the driest (about 17 mm).

Is Aurora cold in winter?

In December the average night-time low is about -7.8 degrees C and the average daytime high about 3.7 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in November to April.

What time zone is Aurora in?

Aurora uses the America/Denver time zone, which is UTC-7:00 in January and UTC-6:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
