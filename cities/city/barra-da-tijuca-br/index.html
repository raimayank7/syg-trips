*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Barra da Tijuca has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 23.3 degrees C and about 1,469 mm of rain falls over the year. February is the hottest month, with days reaching about 28.4 degrees C on average, and August is the coolest, with nights near 17.8 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 4.9 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. December is the wettest month with about 232 mm and August the driest with about 42 mm. December to April feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 37.9 degrees C, in October, and the coldest about 13.9 degrees C, in June. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Barra da Tijuca

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Barra da Tijuca are April to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for November to January. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 10 hours 43 minutes, and around 21 December for about 13 hours 33 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -23.0 degrees).
