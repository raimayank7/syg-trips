Weather and best time to visit Stanley

*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Stanley has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 6.5 degrees C and about 652 mm of rain falls over the year. January is the hottest month, with days reaching about 12.8 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 1.7 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. April is the wettest month with about 68 mm and October the driest with about 42 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 19.9 degrees C, in February, and the coldest about -3.1 degrees C, in June. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Stanley

No month in Stanley is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, January, February and December come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for May to November. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 7 hours 48 minutes, and around 21 December for about 16 hours 40 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -51.7 degrees).

Questions about Stanley

When is the best time to visit Stanley?

No month in Stanley is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, January, February and December come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

How much rain does Stanley get?

About 652 mm a year in this data. April is the wettest month (about 68 mm) and October the driest (about 42 mm).

Is Stanley cold in winter?

In July the average night-time low is about 1.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about 4.2 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Stanley in?

Stanley uses the Atlantic/Stanley time zone, which is UTC-3:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
