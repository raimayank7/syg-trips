*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Aizawl has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 22.9 degrees C and about 2,142 mm of rain falls over the year. April is the hottest month, with days reaching about 31.6 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 9.8 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: May to August bring about 67% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 8 mm. May to September feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 38.1 degrees C, in April, and the coldest about 4.5 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 432 metres, which is 648 metres lower than the city (about 1,080 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 4.2 degrees C too warm for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Aizawl

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Aizawl are December to March. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in December to January; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for May to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 36 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 40 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 23.7 degrees).
