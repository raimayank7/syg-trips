*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Kaohsiung has a warm, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 24.9 degrees C and about 1,765 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 29.7 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 17.5 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: June to September bring about 72% of the year's rain, while February is the driest month with about 27 mm. June to September feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 35.1 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 10.1 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Kaohsiung

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Kaohsiung are October to April. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 31 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 45 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 22.6 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

Questions about Kaohsiung

When is the best time to visit Kaohsiung?

How much rain does Kaohsiung get?

About 1,765 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 436 mm) and February the driest (about 27 mm).

How hot does Kaohsiung get?

The hottest month is May, when the average daily high is about 29.7 degrees C and the average night-time low about 24.5 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Kaohsiung in?

Kaohsiung uses the Asia/Taipei time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
