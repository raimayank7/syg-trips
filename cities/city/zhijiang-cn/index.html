*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Zhijiang has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 17.0 degrees C and about 1,165 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 32.9 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -1.5 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. July is the wettest month with about 187 mm and December the driest with about 21 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to January, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 41.7 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -10.7 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Zhijiang

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Zhijiang are April and September to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to January; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to August; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for July to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 07 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 11 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 30.4 degrees).

How much rain does Zhijiang get?

About 1,165 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 187 mm) and December the driest (about 21 mm).

Is Zhijiang cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -1.5 degrees C and the average daytime high about 8.7 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to January.

What time zone is Zhijiang in?

Zhijiang uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
