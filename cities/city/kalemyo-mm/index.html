*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Kalemyo has a hot, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 26.9 degrees C and about 343 mm of rain falls over the year. April is the hottest month, with days reaching about 37.4 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 11.4 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. June is the wettest month with about 56 mm and February the driest with about 5 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in March to August, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 44.0 degrees C, in May, and the coldest about 6.8 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 393 metres, which is 274 metres higher than the city (about 119 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 1.8 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Kalemyo

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Kalemyo are December to January. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in January; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for March to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 34 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 42 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 23.2 degrees).

How much rain does Kalemyo get?

How hot does Kalemyo get?

The hottest month is April, when the average daily high is about 37.4 degrees C and the average night-time low about 22.1 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

Kalemyo uses the Asia/Yangon time zone, which is UTC+6:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
