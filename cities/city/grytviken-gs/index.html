It lies at 54.28° south, 36.51° west, about 4 metres above sea level. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Grytviken has a cold, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 0.2 degrees C and about 1,094 mm of rain falls over the year. February is the hottest month, with days reaching about 3.9 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near -3.7 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 5.3 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. March is the wettest month with about 117 mm and October the driest with about 70 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in May to November, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 9.4 degrees C, in March, and the coldest about -15.8 degrees C, in July. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

How much rain does Grytviken get?

About 1,094 mm a year in this data. March is the wettest month (about 117 mm) and October the driest (about 70 mm).

Is Grytviken cold in winter?

In July the average night-time low is about -3.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about -1.0 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in May to November.

What time zone is Grytviken in?

Grytviken uses the Atlantic/South Georgia time zone, which is UTC-2:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
