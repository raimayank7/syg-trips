High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Los Angeles has a mild, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 17.3 degrees C and about 332 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.0 degrees C on average, and December is the coolest, with nights near 6.7 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: December to February bring about 64% of the year's rain, while June is the driest month with about 1 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 42.3 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -1.0 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 395 metres, which is 299 metres higher than the city (about 96 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 1.9 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Los Angeles

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Los Angeles are March to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in November to May; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for December to February. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 26 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 53 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 34.1 degrees).

Questions about Los Angeles

When is the best time to visit Los Angeles?

How much rain does Los Angeles get?

About 332 mm a year in this data. February is the wettest month (about 75 mm) and June the driest (about 1 mm).

Is Los Angeles cold in winter?

In December the average night-time low is about 6.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about 16.8 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Los Angeles in?

Los Angeles uses the America/Los Angeles time zone, which is UTC-8:00 in January and UTC-7:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
