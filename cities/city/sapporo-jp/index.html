*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Sapporo has a cool, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 6.1 degrees C and about 1,271 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 24.0 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near -10.0 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 26.8 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is spread through the year. August is the wettest month with about 160 mm and April the driest with about 62 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to April, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 32.8 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -20.7 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 279 metres, which is 253 metres higher than the city (about 26 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 1.6 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Sapporo

No month in Sapporo is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, June, July and August come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 22 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 00 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 43.1 degrees).

Questions about Sapporo

When is the best time to visit Sapporo?

If you must choose, June, July and August come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

How much rain does Sapporo get?

About 1,271 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 160 mm) and April the driest (about 62 mm).

Is Sapporo cold in winter?

In February the average night-time low is about -10.0 degrees C and the average daytime high about -3.3 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in November to April.

What time zone is Sapporo in?

Sapporo uses the Asia/Tokyo time zone, which is UTC+9:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
