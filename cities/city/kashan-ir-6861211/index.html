*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Kashan has a mild, very dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 15.3 degrees C and about 156 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 36.1 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -3.7 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 26.1 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is spread through the year. April is the wettest month with about 28 mm and August the driest with about 0 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to February, so expect frost and, at times, snow. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in July, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 41.6 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -15.1 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 1,618 metres, which is 670 metres higher than the city (about 948 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 4.4 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Kashan

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Kashan are April to May and September to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to February; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for July. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 25 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 53 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 34.0 degrees).

How much rain does Kashan get?

About 156 mm a year in this data. April is the wettest month (about 28 mm) and August the driest (about 0 mm).

Is Kashan cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -3.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about 7.9 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to February.

What time zone is Kashan in?

Kashan uses the Asia/Tehran time zone, which is UTC+3:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
