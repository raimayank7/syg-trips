*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Shijiazhuang has a mild, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 13.7 degrees C and about 533 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 34.0 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -7.5 degrees C. The seasons are strong: the average temperature swings by 30.6 degrees C between the coolest and the warmest month. Rain is concentrated: July to September bring about 62% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 3 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in November to February, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 44.6 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -18.1 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Shijiazhuang

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Shijiazhuang are April to May and September to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for November to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to September; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for June to July. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 48 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 31 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 38.0 degrees).

How much rain does Shijiazhuang get?

About 533 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 136 mm) and January the driest (about 3 mm).

Is Shijiazhuang cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -7.5 degrees C and the average daytime high about 2.6 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in November to February.

What time zone is Shijiazhuang in?

Shijiazhuang uses the Asia/Shanghai time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
