*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Blida has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 17.5 degrees C and about 641 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 34.1 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near 5.1 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. November is the wettest month with about 92 mm and July the driest with about 5 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 42.6 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -0.8 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Blida

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Blida are April to June and September to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in November to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for November to January; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for July to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 39 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 40 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 36.5 degrees).

How much rain does Blida get?

About 641 mm a year in this data. November is the wettest month (about 92 mm) and July the driest (about 5 mm).

Is Blida cold in winter?

In February the average night-time low is about 5.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 13.9 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Blida in?

Blida uses the Africa/Algiers time zone, which is UTC+1:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
