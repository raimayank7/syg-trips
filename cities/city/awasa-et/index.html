High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Awasa has a mild, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 15.7 degrees C and about 1,432 mm of rain falls over the year. February is the hottest month, with days reaching about 25.3 degrees C on average, and December is the coolest, with nights near 7.7 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 3.3 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. April is the wettest month with about 219 mm and January the driest with about 20 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 31.4 degrees C, in February, and the coldest about 2.3 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 2,381 metres, which is 684 metres higher than the city (about 1,697 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 4.4 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Awasa

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Awasa are November to March and June. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in every month; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for April to May. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 12 hours 32 minutes, and around 21 December for about 11 hours 43 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 7.1 degrees).

How much rain does Awasa get?

About 1,432 mm a year in this data. April is the wettest month (about 219 mm) and January the driest (about 20 mm).

Is Awasa cold in winter?

In December the average night-time low is about 7.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about 21.9 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Awasa in?

Awasa uses the Africa/Addis Ababa time zone, which is UTC+3:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
