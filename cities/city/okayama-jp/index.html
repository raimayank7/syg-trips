*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Okayama has a mild, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 15.6 degrees C and about 1,465 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.6 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 1.1 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. July is the wettest month with about 219 mm and January the driest with about 54 mm. July to August feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 35.3 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -3.9 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Okayama

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Okayama are April. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for December to March; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to July and September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 29 minutes, and around 21 December for about 9 hours 50 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 34.6 degrees).

How much rain does Okayama get?

About 1,465 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 219 mm) and January the driest (about 54 mm).

Is Okayama cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 1.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 8.0 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Okayama in?

Okayama uses the Asia/Tokyo time zone, which is UTC+9:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
