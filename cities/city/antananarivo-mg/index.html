Weather and best time to visit Antananarivo

Antananarivo is a city in Madagascar (Africa) with a population of about 1.3 million and is the national capital. It lies at 18.91° south, 47.54° east, about 1,274 metres above sea level. The numbers below show its typical weather month by month, the months that suit sightseeing best, what to pack and how long the days are.

*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Antananarivo has a mild, very wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 16.6 degrees C and about 2,382 mm of rain falls over the year. November is the hottest month, with days reaching about 23.9 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 8.2 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: December to March bring about 65% of the year's rain, while September is the driest month with about 46 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 28.3 degrees C, in October, and the coldest about 3.5 degrees C, in June. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Antananarivo

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Antananarivo are September to October. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. That is a narrow window, so book early if you travel then, and check the weather forecast close to your dates. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in May to October; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for December to March. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 10 hours 59 minutes, and around 21 December for about 13 hours 16 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -18.9 degrees).

How much rain does Antananarivo get?

About 2,382 mm a year in this data. January is the wettest month (about 475 mm) and September the driest (about 46 mm).

How hot does Antananarivo get?

The hottest month is November, when the average daily high is about 23.9 degrees C and the average night-time low about 13.6 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Antananarivo in?

Antananarivo uses the Indian/Antananarivo time zone, which is UTC+3:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
