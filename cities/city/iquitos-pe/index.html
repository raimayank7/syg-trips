*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Iquitos has a hot, very wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 25.6 degrees C and about 3,108 mm of rain falls over the year. September is the hottest month, with days reaching about 28.9 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 22.3 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 0.8 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. March is the wettest month with about 367 mm and August the driest with about 157 mm. every month feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.6 degrees C, in September, and the coldest about 18.0 degrees C, in July. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Iquitos

No month in Iquitos is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, July, August and September come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 11 hours 54 minutes, and around 21 December for about 12 hours 20 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -3.7 degrees).

Questions about Iquitos

When is the best time to visit Iquitos?

No month in Iquitos is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, July, August and September come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

How much rain does Iquitos get?

About 3,108 mm a year in this data. March is the wettest month (about 367 mm) and August the driest (about 157 mm).

How hot does Iquitos get?

The hottest month is September, when the average daily high is about 28.9 degrees C and the average night-time low about 23.0 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Iquitos in?

Iquitos uses the America/Lima time zone, which is UTC-5:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
