High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Victoria de Durango has a mild, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 16.6 degrees C and about 551 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 30.6 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 1.7 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to September bring about 71% of the year's rain, while April is the driest month with about 2 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 36.9 degrees C, in May, and the coldest about -7.5 degrees C, in December. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Victoria de Durango

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Victoria de Durango are April to June and October to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for December to March; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 37 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 39 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 24.0 degrees).

Questions about Victoria de Durango

How much rain does Victoria de Durango get?

About 551 mm a year in this data. July is the wettest month (about 136 mm) and April the driest (about 2 mm).

Is Victoria de Durango cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 1.7 degrees C and the average daytime high about 18.5 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Victoria de Durango in?

Victoria de Durango uses the America/Monterrey time zone, which is UTC-6:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
