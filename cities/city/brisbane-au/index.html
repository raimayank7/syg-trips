*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Brisbane has a warm, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 21.1 degrees C and about 1,100 mm of rain falls over the year. January is the hottest month, with days reaching about 28.7 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 12.1 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. February is the wettest month with about 156 mm and July the driest with about 38 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 39.6 degrees C, in January, and the coldest about 5.6 degrees C, in July. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Brisbane

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Brisbane are April to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for January to February. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 10 hours 24 minutes, and around 21 December for about 13 hours 53 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -27.5 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

Questions about Brisbane

When is the best time to visit Brisbane?

How much rain does Brisbane get?

About 1,100 mm a year in this data. February is the wettest month (about 156 mm) and July the driest (about 38 mm).

How hot does Brisbane get?

The hottest month is January, when the average daily high is about 28.7 degrees C and the average night-time low about 22.1 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Brisbane in?

Brisbane uses the Australia/Brisbane time zone, which is UTC+10:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
