*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Rewari has a hot, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 25.8 degrees C and about 604 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 42.7 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 6.1 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to September bring about 71% of the year's rain, while November is the driest month with about 4 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in April to July, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 48.5 degrees C, in June, and the coldest about 0.1 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Rewari

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Rewari are November to February. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in December to February; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to September; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for April to July. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 56 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 21 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 28.2 degrees).

How much rain does Rewari get?

About 604 mm a year in this data. August is the wettest month (about 171 mm) and November the driest (about 4 mm).

Is Rewari cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 6.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 21.7 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Rewari in?

Rewari uses the Asia/Kolkata time zone, which is UTC+5:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
