*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Puebla has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 16.7 degrees C and about 878 mm of rain falls over the year. April is the hottest month, with days reaching about 27.9 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 6.1 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: June to September bring about 72% of the year's rain, while December is the driest month with about 9 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.3 degrees C, in May, and the coldest about -0.1 degrees C, in November. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Puebla

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Puebla are October to May. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a light jacket or sweater for the evenings in July to April; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for June to September. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 17 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 59 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 19.0 degrees).

Questions about Puebla

When is the best time to visit Puebla?

In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. How much rain does Puebla get?

About 878 mm a year in this data. September is the wettest month (about 169 mm) and December the driest (about 9 mm).

Is Puebla cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 6.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 21.1 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Puebla in?

Puebla uses the America/Mexico City time zone, which is UTC-6:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
