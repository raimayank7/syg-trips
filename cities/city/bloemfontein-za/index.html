High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Bloemfontein has a mild, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 17.3 degrees C and about 489 mm of rain falls over the year. January is the hottest month, with days reaching about 32.1 degrees C on average, and July is the coolest, with nights near 0.4 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: December to March bring about 60% of the year's rain, while July is the driest month with about 5 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 42.4 degrees C, in January, and the coldest about -5.5 degrees C, in August. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Bloemfontein

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Bloemfontein are February to May and September to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for May to August; an umbrella or a light rain jacket for December to March; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for December to January. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 10 hours 17 minutes, and around 21 December for about 14 hours 00 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of -29.1 degrees).

Questions about Bloemfontein

When is the best time to visit Bloemfontein?

How much rain does Bloemfontein get?

About 489 mm a year in this data. January is the wettest month (about 79 mm) and July the driest (about 5 mm).

Is Bloemfontein cold in winter?

In July the average night-time low is about 0.4 degrees C and the average daytime high about 16.7 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Bloemfontein in?

Bloemfontein uses the Africa/Johannesburg time zone, which is UTC+2:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
