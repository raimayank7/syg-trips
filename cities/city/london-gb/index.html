*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

London has a mild, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 10.4 degrees C and about 710 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 22.6 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near 1.1 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. November is the wettest month with about 74 mm and April the driest with about 42 mm.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 34.0 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -8.2 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit London

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in London are June to September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for November to April. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 16 hours 38 minutes, and around 21 December for about 7 hours 49 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 51.5 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

Questions about London

When is the best time to visit London?

In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. How much rain does London get?

About 710 mm a year in this data. November is the wettest month (about 74 mm) and April the driest (about 42 mm).

Is London cold in winter?

In February the average night-time low is about 1.1 degrees C and the average daytime high about 7.4 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is London in?

London uses the Europe/London time zone, which is UTC+0:00 in January and UTC+1:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
