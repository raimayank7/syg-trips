*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Marrakesh has a warm, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 18.8 degrees C and about 291 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 37.7 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 3.9 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. November is the wettest month with about 54 mm and June the driest with about 4 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in July to August, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 44.6 degrees C, in July, and the coldest about -4.2 degrees C, in January. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 837 metres, which is 380 metres higher than the city (about 457 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 2.5 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Marrakesh

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Marrakesh are March to May and October to November. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take warm layers and a jacket for January to February; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for July to August. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 14 hours 13 minutes, and around 21 December for about 10 hours 05 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 31.6 degrees).

Questions about Marrakesh

When is the best time to visit Marrakesh?

How much rain does Marrakesh get?

About 291 mm a year in this data. November is the wettest month (about 54 mm) and June the driest (about 4 mm).

Is Marrakesh cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about 3.9 degrees C and the average daytime high about 16.5 degrees C. It does not usually freeze at night on average, but cold spells happen.

What time zone is Marrakesh in?

Marrakesh uses the Africa/Casablanca time zone, which is UTC+0:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
