Weather and best time to visit Sarajevo

Sarajevo is a city in Bosnia and Herzegovina (Europe) with a population of about 696,731 and is the national capital. It lies at 43.85° north, 18.36° east, about 509 metres above sea level. *Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Sarajevo has a cool, moderately wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 8.7 degrees C and about 899 mm of rain falls over the year. August is the hottest month, with days reaching about 26.1 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near -5.5 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. November is the wettest month with about 91 mm and July the driest with about 54 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to March, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 36.9 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -24.8 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme. Height warning: the weather grid cell for this city has an average height of about 881 metres, which is 372 metres higher than the city (about 509 metres). Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C per 1,000 metres, so the temperatures in the table may be about 2.4 degrees C too cool for the city itself. Rain and humidity can also differ. We have not adjusted the figures; treat them as a guide.

Best time to visit Sarajevo

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Sarajevo are June to September. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to March. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 15 hours 28 minutes, and around 21 December for about 8 hours 54 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 43.8 degrees).

Questions about Sarajevo

When is the best time to visit Sarajevo?

How much rain does Sarajevo get?

About 899 mm a year in this data. November is the wettest month (about 91 mm) and July the driest (about 54 mm).

Is Sarajevo cold in winter?

In January the average night-time low is about -5.5 degrees C and the average daytime high about 2.1 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to March.

What time zone is Sarajevo in?

Sarajevo uses the Europe/Sarajevo time zone, which is UTC+1:00 in January and UTC+2:00 in July (clocks change for summer time).
