*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Reykjavik has a cool, wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 4.6 degrees C and about 1,638 mm of rain falls over the year. July is the hottest month, with days reaching about 13.5 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near -1.8 degrees C. Rain is spread through the year. September is the wettest month with about 181 mm and June the driest with about 83 mm. Night-time averages fall to freezing or below in December to March, so expect frost and, at times, snow.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 22.8 degrees C, in August, and the coldest about -14.2 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Reykjavik

No month in Reykjavik is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, June, July and August come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take a proper winter coat, gloves and a warm hat for December to March. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 21 hours 08 minutes, and around 21 December for about 4 hours 07 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 64.1 degrees).

Check before you travel. Climate averages describe a typical year from long records (NASA POWER data, refreshed November 2026). They are not a forecast, and they do not cover visas, prices, safety or closures. Check the official sources for those and the weather forecast for your dates.

Questions about Reykjavik

When is the best time to visit Reykjavik?

How much rain does Reykjavik get?

About 1,638 mm a year in this data. September is the wettest month (about 181 mm) and June the driest (about 83 mm).

Is Reykjavik cold in winter?

In February the average night-time low is about -1.8 degrees C and the average daytime high about 1.9 degrees C. Nights average at or below freezing in December to March.

What time zone is Reykjavik in?

Reykjavik uses the Atlantic/Reykjavik time zone, which is UTC+0:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
