*Sunshine here is the solar energy reaching the ground in kWh per square metre per day: higher means clearer, sunnier days. High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Bandar Seri Begawan has a hot, very wet climate in this data: the yearly average is 27.3 degrees C and about 2,991 mm of rain falls over the year. May is the hottest month, with days reaching about 29.4 degrees C on average, and February is the coolest, with nights near 25.0 degrees C. The seasons change little: the warmest and coolest months differ by only 1.5 degrees C in their average temperature. Rain is spread through the year. December is the wettest month with about 333 mm and February the driest with about 143 mm. every month feel muggy: the air is warm and the average humidity is 80% or higher.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 32.1 degrees C, in April, and the coldest about 23.0 degrees C, in February. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Bandar Seri Begawan

No month in Bandar Seri Begawan is both mild and dry by the rules used here (average high 18 to 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, 120 mm of rain or less, and no heavy humidity). If you must choose, February, March and April come closest. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

Questions about Bandar Seri Begawan

When is the best time to visit Bandar Seri Begawan?

How much rain does Bandar Seri Begawan get?

About 2,991 mm a year in this data. December is the wettest month (about 333 mm) and February the driest (about 143 mm).

How hot does Bandar Seri Begawan get?

The hottest month is May, when the average daily high is about 29.4 degrees C and the average night-time low about 26.7 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Bandar Seri Begawan in?

Bandar Seri Begawan uses the Asia/Brunei time zone, which is UTC+8:00 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
