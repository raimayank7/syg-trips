High and low are estimates: the mean temperature of the month plus and minus half the average gap between the highest and lowest temperature of a day (NASA POWER). Rain is the total for the month.

Ballari has a hot, dry climate in this data: the yearly average is 26.5 degrees C and about 587 mm of rain falls over the year. April is the hottest month, with days reaching about 38.7 degrees C on average, and January is the coolest, with nights near 15.6 degrees C. Rain is concentrated: July to October bring about 64% of the year's rain, while January is the driest month with about 2 mm. Daytime highs average 35 degrees C or more in March to May, which is hard going for walking tours in the afternoon.

Averages hide the extremes. In this dataset (hourly model values for 2001 to 2025 over the grid cell around the city) the hottest reading was about 43.1 degrees C, in May, and the coldest about 10.6 degrees C, in November. Real thermometers in a street can read a few degrees more extreme.

Best time to visit Ballari

Judged by weather alone, the most comfortable months for sightseeing in Ballari are July to December. In those months the average high is between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights stay above 5 degrees C, rainfall is 120 mm or less and the air is not very humid. This only measures weather. Crowds, prices, school holidays, festivals and closures also decide the best time, so look at those too.

Rule used, the same for every city: a month counts as comfortable when the average high is 18 to 31 degrees C, the average night low is at least 5, the rain is 120 mm or less and the average humidity is 82% or less. It is a rule, not an opinion.

What to pack

From the monthly numbers, plan to take an umbrella or a light rain jacket for July to October; light cotton clothes, a hat, sunscreen and a water bottle for March to May. A packing list for your kind of trip is on the trips site: packing lists.

Day length and sunshine

Day length changes with the season. Around 21 June the sun is up for about 13 hours 01 minutes, and around 21 December for about 11 hours 14 minutes (standard solar formula, accurate to a few minutes, from the city's latitude of 15.1 degrees).

How much rain does Ballari get?

About 587 mm a year in this data. September is the wettest month (about 120 mm) and January the driest (about 2 mm).

How hot does Ballari get?

The hottest month is April, when the average daily high is about 38.7 degrees C and the average night-time low about 24.5 degrees C. Individual days can be hotter than the average.

What time zone is Ballari in?

Ballari uses the Asia/Kolkata time zone, which is UTC+5:30 in January and the same in July (no clock change).
