Weather in South Korea: 32 cities compared

South Korea is in Asia. Its capital is Seoul and about 51.7 million people live in the country, with Won as the currency. This page covers the 32 cities of South Korea in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Jeju City has the warmest year (average 16.2 degrees C) and Wonju the coolest (8.8 degrees C). Jeju City is the wettest with about 1,578 mm of rain a year and Pohang the driest with about 1,187 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), October is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 30 of 32.
