Weather in Saudi Arabia: 13 cities compared

Saudi Arabia is in Asia. Its capital is Riyadh and about 37.0 million people live in the country, with Rial as the currency. This page covers the 13 cities of Saudi Arabia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Jeddah has the warmest year (average 29.9 degrees C) and Tabuk the coolest (21.6 degrees C). Khamis Mushait is the wettest with about 60 mm of rain a year and Madinah the driest with about 22 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), December is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 12 of 13.
