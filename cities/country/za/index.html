Weather in South Africa: 25 cities compared

South Africa is in Africa. Its capital is Pretoria and about 64.7 million people live in the country, with Rand as the currency. This page covers the 25 cities of South Africa in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Durban has the warmest year (average 22.3 degrees C) and Botshabelo the coolest (15.7 degrees C). Pietermaritzburg is the wettest with about 1,169 mm of rain a year and Gqeberha the driest with about 436 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), March is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 25 of 25.
