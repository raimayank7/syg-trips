Weather in Spain: 13 cities compared

Spain is in Europe. Its capital is Madrid and about 49.4 million people live in the country, with Euro as the currency. This page covers the 13 cities of Spain in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Las Palmas de Gran Canaria has the warmest year (average 20.2 degrees C) and Valladolid the coolest (12.5 degrees C). Bilbao is the wettest with about 1,003 mm of rain a year and Las Palmas de Gran Canaria the driest with about 157 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), October is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 13 of 13.
