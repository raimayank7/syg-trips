Weather in Morocco: 14 cities compared

Morocco is in Africa. Its capital is Rabat and about 38.4 million people live in the country, with Dirham as the currency. This page covers the 14 cities of Morocco in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Agadir has the warmest year (average 21.0 degrees C) and Oujda the coolest (15.9 degrees C). Fes is the wettest with about 714 mm of rain a year and Agadir the driest with about 266 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 14 of 14.
