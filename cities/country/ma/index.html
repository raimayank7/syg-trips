Weather in Morocco: 14 cities compared

Morocco is in Africa. Its capital is Rabat and about 38.4 million people live in the country, with Dirham as the currency. This page covers the 14 cities of Morocco in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Agadir has the warmest year (average 21.0 degrees C) and Oujda the coolest (15.9 degrees C). Fes is the wettest with about 714 mm of rain a year and Agadir the driest with about 266 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 14 of 14.

Morocco month by month

Counting the 14 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 1 in Jan, 13 in Apr, 7 in Jul, 14 in Oct. In May and October every one of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Marrakesh in July, with an average high of 37.7 degrees C, while Kenitra is 14.2 degrees C cooler that month (23.5).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Agadir and Oujda, about 860 km apart in a straight line. Tangier is the most northern of the cities (35.8 degrees) and Agadir the most southern (30.4 degrees), so the group spans about 590 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Oujda lies about 549 metres above sea level and Kenitra about 13 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 3.5 degrees C. All of them use the Africa/Casablanca time zone (UTC+0:00 in January).

Morocco compared with other countries

Across its 14 cities, Morocco has an average yearly temperature of 18.6 degrees C and about 480 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 47 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 63 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Africa, among 23 such countries, it is number 21 for warmth and number 19 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
