Weather in Nigeria: 52 cities compared

Nigeria is in Africa. Its capital is Abuja and about 237.5 million people live in the country, with Naira as the currency. This page covers the 52 cities of Nigeria in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Maiduguri has the warmest year (average 28.4 degrees C) and Jos the coolest (23.6 degrees C). Calabar is the wettest with about 2,930 mm of rain a year and Maiduguri the driest with about 546 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), January is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 40 of 52.
