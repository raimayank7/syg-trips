Weather in Nigeria: 52 cities compared

Nigeria is in Africa. Its capital is Abuja and about 237.5 million people live in the country, with Naira as the currency. This page covers the 52 cities of Nigeria in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Maiduguri has the warmest year (average 28.4 degrees C) and Jos the coolest (23.6 degrees C). Calabar is the wettest with about 2,930 mm of rain a year and Maiduguri the driest with about 546 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), January is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 40 of 52.

Nigeria month by month

Counting the 52 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 40 in Jan, 1 in Apr, 0 in Jul, 5 in Oct. In December to January at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. In May and July to August none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Maiduguri in April, with an average high of 41.3 degrees C, while Ogbia is 12.4 degrees C cooler that month (28.9).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Maiduguri and Ajegunle, about 1,230 km apart in a straight line. Sokoto is the most northern of the cities (13.1 degrees) and Ogbia the most southern (4.7 degrees), so the group spans about 930 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Jos lies about 1,186 metres above sea level and Ajegunle about 2 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 7.7 degrees C. All of them use the Africa/Lagos time zone (UTC+1:00 in January).

Nigeria compared with other countries

Across its 52 cities, Nigeria has an average yearly temperature of 25.8 degrees C and about 1,530 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 16 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 9 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Africa, among 23 such countries, it is number 6 for warmth and number 2 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
