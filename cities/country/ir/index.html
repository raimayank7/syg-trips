Weather in Iran: 28 cities compared

Iran is in Asia. Its capital is Tehran and about 92.4 million people live in the country, with Rial as the currency. This page covers the 28 cities of Iran in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Bandar Abbas has the warmest year (average 27.8 degrees C) and Karaj the coolest (7.2 degrees C). Rasht is the wettest with about 1,019 mm of rain a year and Yazd the driest with about 54 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 23 of 28.

Iran month by month

Counting the 28 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 3 in Jan, 13 in Apr, 5 in Jul, 20 in Oct. In May and September to October at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. The hottest city-month of the group is Ahvaz in July, with an average high of 48.5 degrees C, while Karaj is 21.6 degrees C cooler that month (26.9).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Orumiyeh and Zahedan, about 1,710 km apart in a straight line. Ardabil is the most northern of the cities (38.2 degrees) and Bandar Abbas the most southern (27.2 degrees), so the group spans about 1,230 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Hamadan lies about 1,820 metres above sea level and Rasht about 4 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 11.8 degrees C. All of them use the Asia/Tehran time zone (UTC+3:30 in January).

Iran compared with other countries

Across its 28 cities, Iran has an average yearly temperature of 15.3 degrees C and about 290 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 58 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 70 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Asia, among 29 such countries, it is number 21 for warmth and number 20 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
