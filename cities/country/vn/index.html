Weather in Vietnam: 35 cities compared

Vietnam is in Asia. Its capital is Hanoi and about 101.6 million people live in the country, with Dong as the currency. This page covers the 35 cities of Vietnam in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Rach Gia has the warmest year (average 27.8 degrees C) and Bac Giang the coolest (22.6 degrees C). Da Nang is the wettest with about 2,209 mm of rain a year and Buon Ma Thuot the driest with about 926 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), December is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 28 of 35.

Vietnam month by month

Counting the 35 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 16 in Jan, 15 in Apr, 3 in Jul, 1 in Oct. In December at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. In August to September none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Ho Chi Minh City in April, with an average high of 36.7 degrees C, while Haiphong is 9.1 degrees C cooler that month (27.6).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Rach Gia and Thai Nguyen, about 1,290 km apart in a straight line. Thai Nguyen is the most northern of the cities (21.6 degrees) and Rach Gia the most southern (10.0 degrees), so the group spans about 1,290 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Buon Ma Thuot lies about 459 metres above sea level and Can Tho about 3 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 3.0 degrees C. They are spread over 2 time zones: Asia/Bangkok, Asia/Ho Chi Minh.

Vietnam compared with other countries

Across its 35 cities, Vietnam has an average yearly temperature of 25.5 degrees C and about 1,540 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 18 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 8 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Asia, among 29 such countries, it is number 8 for warmth and number 7 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
