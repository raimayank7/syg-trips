Weather in Colombia: 24 cities compared

Colombia is in South America. Its capital is Bogota and about 53.4 million people live in the country, with Peso as the currency. This page covers the 24 cities of Colombia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Cartagena has the warmest year (average 28.1 degrees C) and Pasto the coolest (13.2 degrees C). Villavicencio is the wettest with about 2,438 mm of rain a year and Valledupar the driest with about 717 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), January is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 12 of 24.
