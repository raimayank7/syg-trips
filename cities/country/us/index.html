Weather in United States: 74 cities compared

United States is in North America. Its capital is Washington and about 341.8 million people live in the country, with Dollar as the currency. This page covers the 74 cities of United States in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Miami has the warmest year (average 25.4 degrees C) and Minneapolis the coolest (6.9 degrees C). New Orleans is the wettest with about 1,532 mm of rain a year and Las Vegas the driest with about 147 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), September is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 52 of 74.
