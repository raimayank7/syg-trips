Weather in Philippines: 44 cities compared

Philippines is in Asia. Its capital is Manila and about 116.8 million people live in the country, with Peso as the currency. This page covers the 44 cities of Philippines in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Davao has the warmest year (average 27.5 degrees C) and Iligan the coolest (23.0 degrees C). Butuan is the wettest with about 3,423 mm of rain a year and Zamboanga the driest with about 1,638 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), March is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 38 of 44.
