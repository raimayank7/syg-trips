Weather in Philippines: 44 cities compared

Philippines is in Asia. Its capital is Manila and about 116.8 million people live in the country, with Peso as the currency. This page covers the 44 cities of Philippines in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Davao has the warmest year (average 27.5 degrees C) and Iligan the coolest (23.0 degrees C). Butuan is the wettest with about 3,423 mm of rain a year and Zamboanga the driest with about 1,638 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), March is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 38 of 44.

Philippines month by month

Counting the 44 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 5 in Jan, 10 in Apr, 0 in Jul, 0 in Oct. In March at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. In May to November none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Tarlac City in April, with an average high of 35.9 degrees C, while Iligan is 6.9 degrees C cooler that month (29.0).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are General Santos and Tarlac City, about 1,150 km apart in a straight line. Cabanatuan City is the most northern of the cities (15.5 degrees) and General Santos the most southern (6.1 degrees), so the group spans about 1,040 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Iligan City lies about 282 metres above sea level and Budta about 4 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 1.8 degrees C. All of them use the Asia/Manila time zone (UTC+8:00 in January).

Philippines compared with other countries

Across its 44 cities, Philippines has an average yearly temperature of 26.0 degrees C and about 2,630 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 14 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 1 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Asia, among 29 such countries, it is number 5 for warmth and number 1 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
