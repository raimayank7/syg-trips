Weather in China: 395 cities compared

This page covers the 395 cities of China in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Wanning has the warmest year (average 25.2 degrees C) and Daxing'anling the coolest (-2.2 degrees C). Yangjiang is the wettest with about 2,119 mm of rain a year and Gujangbagh the driest with about 63 mm.
