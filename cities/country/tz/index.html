Weather in Tanzania: 12 cities compared

Tanzania is in Africa. Its capital is Dodoma and about 70.5 million people live in the country, with Shilling as the currency. This page covers the 12 cities of Tanzania in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Zanzibar has the warmest year (average 27.2 degrees C) and Mbeya the coolest (16.5 degrees C). Mbeya is the wettest with about 1,519 mm of rain a year and Dodoma the driest with about 728 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), June is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 12 of 12.

Tanzania month by month

Counting the 12 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 6 in Jan, 3 in Apr, 12 in Jul, 7 in Oct. In June to August every one of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Kahama in September, with an average high of 32.7 degrees C, while Mbeya is 8.1 degrees C cooler that month (24.6).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Dar es Salaam and Geita, about 890 km apart in a straight line. Mwanza is the most northern of the cities (-2.5 degrees) and Mbeya the most southern (-8.9 degrees), so the group spans about 710 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Sumbawanga lies about 1,833 metres above sea level and Zanzibar about 14 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 11.8 degrees C. All of them use the Africa/Dar es Salaam time zone (UTC+3:00 in January).

Tanzania compared with other countries

Across its 12 cities, Tanzania has an average yearly temperature of 23.2 degrees C and about 1,070 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 30 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 24 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Africa, among 23 such countries, it is number 13 for warmth and number 9 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
