Weather in Brazil: 97 cities compared

Brazil is in South America. Its capital is Brasilia and about 212.8 million people live in the country, with Real as the currency. This page covers the 97 cities of Brazil in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Teresina has the warmest year (average 28.2 degrees C) and Caxias do Sul the coolest (17.2 degrees C). Ananindeua is the wettest with about 2,623 mm of rain a year and Petrolina the driest with about 431 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), August is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 75 of 97.
