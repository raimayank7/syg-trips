Weather in Japan: 88 cities compared

Japan is in Asia. Its capital is Tokyo and about 123.4 million people live in the country, with Yen as the currency. This page covers the 88 cities of Japan in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Naha has the warmest year (average 23.9 degrees C) and Asahikawa the coolest (4.9 degrees C). Miyazaki is the wettest with about 2,772 mm of rain a year and Asahikawa the driest with about 1,045 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), October is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 8 of 88.
