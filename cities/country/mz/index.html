Weather in Mozambique: 7 cities compared

Mozambique is in Africa. Its capital is Maputo and about 35.6 million people live in the country, with Metical as the currency. This page covers the 7 cities of Mozambique in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Quelimane has the warmest year (average 25.9 degrees C) and Chimoio the coolest (22.6 degrees C). Nampula is the wettest with about 1,383 mm of rain a year and Maputo the driest with about 778 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), June is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 7 of 7.

Mozambique month by month

Counting the 7 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 0 in Jan, 5 in Apr, 7 in Jul, 3 in Oct. In June to July every one of them is comfortable. In December to January none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Tete in October, with an average high of 37.6 degrees C, while Beira is 10.2 degrees C cooler that month (27.4).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Matola and Nampula, about 1,400 km apart in a straight line. Nampula is the most northern of the cities (-15.1 degrees) and Maputo the most southern (-26.0 degrees), so the group spans about 1,200 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Chimoio lies about 709 metres above sea level and Beira about 6 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 4.6 degrees C. All of them use the Africa/Maputo time zone (UTC+2:00 in January).

Mozambique compared with other countries

Across its 7 cities, Mozambique has an average yearly temperature of 24.3 degrees C and about 1,000 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 25 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 28 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Africa, among 23 such countries, it is number 9 for warmth and number 10 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
