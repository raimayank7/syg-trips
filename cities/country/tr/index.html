Weather in Turkey: 49 cities compared

Turkey is in Asia. Its capital is Ankara and about 85.9 million people live in the country, with Lira as the currency. This page covers the 49 cities of Turkey in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Alanya has the warmest year (average 20.7 degrees C) and Erzurum the coolest (4.3 degrees C). Alanya is the wettest with about 939 mm of rain a year and Aksaray the driest with about 344 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 47 of 49.
