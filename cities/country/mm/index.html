Weather in Myanmar: 6 cities compared

Myanmar is in Asia. Its capital is Nay Pyi Taw and about 54.9 million people live in the country, with Kyat as the currency. This page covers the 6 cities of Myanmar in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Yangon has the warmest year (average 28.1 degrees C) and Kalemyo the coolest (26.9 degrees C). Mawlamyine is the wettest with about 2,215 mm of rain a year and Mandalay the driest with about 261 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), January is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 4 of 6.
