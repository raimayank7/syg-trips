Weather in Malaysia: 34 cities compared

Malaysia is in Asia. Its capital is Kuala Lumpur and about 36.0 million people live in the country, with Ringgit as the currency. This page covers the 34 cities of Malaysia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Miri has the warmest year (average 27.9 degrees C) and Ipoh the coolest (23.6 degrees C). Kuching is the wettest with about 3,803 mm of rain a year and Malacca the driest with about 2,236 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), February is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 5 of 34.
