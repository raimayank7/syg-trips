Weather in Ukraine: 20 cities compared

Ukraine is in Europe. Its capital is Kyiv and about 39.0 million people live in the country, with Hryvnia as the currency. This page covers the 20 cities of Ukraine in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Sevastopol has the warmest year (average 12.5 degrees C) and Kharkiv the coolest (7.9 degrees C). Lviv is the wettest with about 734 mm of rain a year and Mykolayiv the driest with about 450 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 20 of 20.
