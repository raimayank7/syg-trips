Weather in Mexico: 73 cities compared

Mexico is in North America. Its capital is Mexico City and about 131.9 million people live in the country, with Peso as the currency. This page covers the 73 cities of Mexico in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Merida has the warmest year (average 26.5 degrees C) and Iztapalapa the coolest (13.6 degrees C). Tapachula de Cordova y Ordonez is the wettest with about 2,569 mm of rain a year and Mexicali the driest with about 125 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), March is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 69 of 73.

Mexico month by month

Counting the 73 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 33 in Jan, 50 in Apr, 9 in Jul, 60 in Oct. In February to May and October to November at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. The hottest city-month of the group is Mexicali in July, with an average high of 43.3 degrees C, while Mexico City is 22.7 degrees C cooler that month (20.6).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Tijuana and Cancun, about 3,230 km apart in a straight line. Mexicali is the most northern of the cities (32.6 degrees) and Tapachula de Cordova y Ordonez the most southern (14.9 degrees), so the group spans about 1,970 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Toluca lies about 2,671 metres above sea level and Mazatlan about 1 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 17.4 degrees C. They are spread over 10 time zones: America/Cancun, America/Chihuahua, America/Ciudad Juarez, America/Hermosillo, America/Matamoros, America/Mazatlan, America/Merida, America/Mexico City, America/Monterrey, America/Tijuana.

Mexico compared with other countries

Across its 73 cities, Mexico has an average yearly temperature of 18.5 degrees C and about 830 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 48 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 38 for rain (1 is wettest). Within North America, among 7 such countries, it is number 5 for warmth and number 4 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
