Weather in Mexico: 73 cities compared

Mexico is in North America. Its capital is Mexico City and about 131.9 million people live in the country, with Peso as the currency. This page covers the 73 cities of Mexico in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Merida has the warmest year (average 26.5 degrees C) and Iztapalapa the coolest (13.6 degrees C). Tapachula de Cordova y Ordonez is the wettest with about 2,569 mm of rain a year and Mexicali the driest with about 125 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), March is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 69 of 73.
