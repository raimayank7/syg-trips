Weather in Canada: 19 cities compared

Canada is in North America. Its capital is Ottawa and about 41.7 million people live in the country, with Dollar as the currency. This page covers the 19 cities of Canada in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Vancouver has the warmest year (average 10.6 degrees C) and Quebec the coolest (3.0 degrees C). Halifax is the wettest with about 1,300 mm of rain a year and Edmonton the driest with about 520 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), June is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 18 of 19.
