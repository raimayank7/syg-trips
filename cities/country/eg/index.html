Weather in Egypt: 26 cities compared

Egypt is in Africa. Its capital is Cairo and about 118.4 million people live in the country, with Pound as the currency. This page covers the 26 cities of Egypt in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Kom Ombo has the warmest year (average 26.4 degrees C) and Ismailia the coolest (21.2 degrees C). Port Said is the wettest with about 154 mm of rain a year and Aswan the driest with about 0 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), November is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 26 of 26.
