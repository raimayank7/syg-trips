Weather in Ivory Coast: 8 cities compared

Ivory Coast is in Africa. Its capital is Yamoussoukro and about 32.7 million people live in the country, with Franc as the currency. This page covers the 8 cities of Ivory Coast in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Korhogo has the warmest year (average 26.9 degrees C) and Daloa the coolest (25.9 degrees C). San-Pedro is the wettest with about 1,876 mm of rain a year and Korhogo the driest with about 995 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), December is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 3 of 8.
