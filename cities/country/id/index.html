Weather in Indonesia: 48 cities compared

Indonesia is in Asia. Its capital is Jakarta and about 285.7 million people live in the country, with Rupiah as the currency. This page covers the 48 cities of Indonesia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Surabaya has the warmest year (average 28.0 degrees C) and Bandung the coolest (21.4 degrees C). Padang is the wettest with about 3,532 mm of rain a year and Kupang the driest with about 1,287 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), August is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 26 of 48.
