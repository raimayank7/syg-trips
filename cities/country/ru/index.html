Weather in Russia: 65 cities compared

Russia is in Europe. Its capital is Moscow and about 143.5 million people live in the country, with Ruble as the currency. This page covers the 65 cities of Russia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Makhachkala has the warmest year (average 13.7 degrees C) and Surgut the coolest (-2.7 degrees C). Sochi is the wettest with about 1,287 mm of rain a year and Astrakhan the driest with about 217 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), June is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 62 of 65.

Russia month by month

Counting the 65 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 0 in Jan, 0 in Apr, 56 in Jul, 1 in Oct. In May to August at least half of them are comfortable, which makes it the safest stretch for a trip that visits several cities. In November to April none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Astrakhan in July, with an average high of 34.8 degrees C, while Arkhangel'sk is 14.6 degrees C cooler that month (20.2).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Vladivostok and Kaliningrad, about 7,360 km apart in a straight line. Arkhangel'sk is the most northern of the cities (64.5 degrees) and Makhachkala the most southern (43.0 degrees), so the group spans about 2,390 km from north to south. Heights differ too: Chita lies about 690 metres above sea level and Astrakhan about -12 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 4.6 degrees C. They are spread over 18 time zones: Asia/Barnaul, Asia/Chita, Asia/Irkutsk, Asia/Krasnoyarsk, Asia/Novokuznetsk, Asia/Novosibirsk, Asia/Omsk, Asia/Tomsk, Asia/Vladivostok, Asia/Yekaterinburg, Europe/Astrakhan, Europe/Kaliningrad, Europe/Kirov, Europe/Moscow, Europe/Samara, Europe/Saratov, Europe/Ulyanovsk, Europe/Volgograd.

Russia compared with other countries

Across its 65 cities, Russia has an average yearly temperature of 4.2 degrees C and about 630 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 83 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 52 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Europe, among 14 such countries, it is number 14 for warmth and number 10 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.
