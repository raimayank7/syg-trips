Weather in Russia: 65 cities compared

Russia is in Europe. Its capital is Moscow and about 143.5 million people live in the country, with Ruble as the currency. This page covers the 65 cities of Russia in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Makhachkala has the warmest year (average 13.7 degrees C) and Surgut the coolest (-2.7 degrees C). Sochi is the wettest with about 1,287 mm of rain a year and Astrakhan the driest with about 217 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), June is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 62 of 65.
