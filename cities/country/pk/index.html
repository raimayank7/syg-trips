Weather in Pakistan: 45 cities compared

Pakistan is in Asia. Its capital is Islamabad and about 255.2 million people live in the country, with Rupee as the currency. This page covers the 45 cities of Pakistan in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Larkana has the warmest year (average 29.1 degrees C) and Muzaffarabad the coolest (10.7 degrees C). Rawalpindi is the wettest with about 1,002 mm of rain a year and Larkana the driest with about 127 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), February is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 41 of 45.
