Weather in Azerbaijan: 3 cities compared

Azerbaijan is in Asia. Its capital is Baku and about 10.2 million people live in the country, with Manat as the currency. This page covers the 3 cities of Azerbaijan in our data and compares their weather. Among them, Baku has the warmest year (average 15.3 degrees C) and Ganja the coolest (10.3 degrees C). Ganja is the wettest with about 319 mm of rain a year and Baku the driest with about 160 mm. By the weather rules we use (see the guide to how we pick months), May is the month when most of these cities are comfortable: 3 of 3.

Azerbaijan month by month

Counting the 3 cities, the number that pass the comfort rule is 0 in Jan, 0 in Apr, 2 in Jul, 2 in Oct. In May to June and September every one of them is comfortable. In November to April none of them is comfortable. The hottest city-month of the group is Sumgayit in July, with an average high of 31.3 degrees C, while Baku is 3.5 degrees C cooler that month (27.8).

How far apart the cities are

The two cities farthest from each other are Baku and Ganja, about 300 km apart in a straight line. Ganja is the most northern of the cities (40.7 degrees) and Baku the most southern (40.4 degrees), which is little difference. Heights differ too: Ganja lies about 409 metres above sea level and Baku about -23 metres, a gap that alone cools the higher city by roughly 2.8 degrees C. All of them use the Asia/Baku time zone (UTC+4:00 in January).

Azerbaijan compared with other countries

Across its 3 cities, Azerbaijan has an average yearly temperature of 13.4 degrees C and about 230 mm of rain a year. Among the 83 countries on this site with at least three cities, that puts it at number 67 for warmth (1 is warmest) and number 76 for rain (1 is wettest). Within Asia, among 29 such countries, it is number 26 for warmth and number 24 for rain. The figures are the plain average of its cities, not weighted by population.

The cities one by one

  • Baku: hottest in August (average high 28.5 degrees C), coolest in February (average low 4.1 degrees C), wettest in November (about 25 mm). Comfortable by our rule in May to October.
  • Sumgayit: hottest in July (average high 31.3 degrees C), coolest in January (average low 0.2 degrees C), wettest in October (about 27 mm). Comfortable by our rule in May to June and September to October.
  • Ganja: hottest in July (average high 28.5 degrees C), coolest in January (average low -6.0 degrees C), wettest in May (about 52 mm). Comfortable by our rule in May to September.

The capital, Baku, is one of these cities, so the capital's own page is the quickest way to see the weather where most official business is done.
