Where is the weather comfortable in December?

1019 of the 2,491 cities in our data have comfortable weather in December by our rules: an average daily high between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, at most 120 mm of rain in the month and no heavy humidity. The lists below group them by continent, largest cities first (up to 30 each). Among them, 492 are in India, such as Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad. Comfortable does not mean quiet or cheap: peak seasons, festivals and school holidays change crowds and prices, so use this list to shortlist places and then check the rest.
