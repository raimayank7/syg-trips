Where is the weather comfortable in October?

1116 of the 2,491 cities in our data have comfortable weather in October by our rules: an average daily high between 18 and 31 degrees C, nights above 5 degrees C, at most 120 mm of rain in the month and no heavy humidity. The lists below group them by continent, largest cities first (up to 30 each). Among them, 224 are in India, such as Mumbai, Hyderabad, Pune, Navi Mumbai. Comfortable does not mean quiet or cheap: peak seasons, festivals and school holidays change crowds and prices, so use this list to shortlist places and then check the rest.
