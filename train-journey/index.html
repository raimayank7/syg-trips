Train journey packing list

A long train journey is part of the holiday if you are comfortable, and a trial if you are not. The things that decide which are small: a chain for the bag, something to sleep under, your own food and water, and a charged power bank.

Tips for this kind of trip

  • Keep one small bag with you at all times holding tickets, identity cards, medicines, phone, charger, cash and valuables. Fasten the rest under the berth.
  • Do not accept food or drink from strangers, however friendly.
  • Charging points are few. Board with the phone and power bank full.
  • Note the coach position on the platform before the train arrives; long trains stop for a short time at small stations.
  • With elders, request lower berths when booking and check the railway's current rules and concessions on its official website. Reach the station early.
  • Set an alarm for half an hour before your station if it arrives at night, and tell a co-passenger or the attendant.
  • Book tickets and check running status only on the railway's official website or app.

Questions

What food is best to carry on a train?

Dry or semi-dry home food that keeps without a fridge, such as parathas, puris, dry vegetable dishes, lemon rice and theplas, with fruit and biscuits. Avoid curd and gravy dishes on long journeys.

How do I keep luggage safe on a train?

Chain bags to the loops under the berth, keep valuables in a small bag that never leaves you, and do not leave phones charging unattended.
