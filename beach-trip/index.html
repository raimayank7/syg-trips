Beach trip packing list

A beach trip needs less clothing than most trips and more protection: from the sun, from salt water and from sand getting into everything. Pack light cottons, and give the space you save to sun care and a way to keep the phone and money dry.

Tips for this kind of trip

  • Reapply sunscreen every two hours and after every swim. The sea breeze hides the heat, and most sunburn happens on the first day.
  • Swim only where others are swimming and where lifeguards or flags mark the safe area. Ask local people about currents before entering unfamiliar water, and keep children within arm's reach.
  • Salt and sand ruin phones and cameras. Keep electronics in a zip pouch even when you are not near the water.
  • Drink more water than you think you need, and go easy on alcohol in the sun.
  • Rinse swimwear in fresh water each evening so that it dries by morning and lasts longer.
  • Leave valuables in the room. Carry only a little cash and one card to the beach.

Questions

What should I not take to the beach?

Jewellery, a wallet full of cards, leather shoes and anything that cannot get wet or sandy. Take a little cash, one card and your phone in a waterproof pouch.

How many clothes do I need for a beach holiday?

Fewer than you expect: two sets of swimwear, a few light cotton tops and shorts, and one set of proper clothes for evenings or the journey.
