About these guides

Planning a trip is mostly a matter of doing a few things in the right order and agreeing on money early. These guides set out that order plainly, for the kinds of journey most families and groups of friends actually make: a holiday together, a pilgrimage with parents, a first flight, a long drive.

They are written from ordinary experience, not from a brochure. They name no hotels, agents or apps and quote no prices; where a rule or a charge matters, they tell you which official source to check, because those change and differ from place to place.

Start with the one you need

  • Going as a group? How to plan a group trip, then how to split costs fairly.
  • Taking parents or grandparents? Travelling with elders.
  • Flying for the first time? First flight, step by step.
  • Leaving tomorrow? Before you leave home.
  • Worried about being cheated or losing things? Travel safety.

Each guide links to the tool that goes with it: the budget planner, the cost splitter, the packing list maker or the fuel calculator.

Do the guides recommend places or hotels?

No. They cover how to plan and travel, whatever the destination.

Are the guides only for travel in India?

They are written with Indian travellers in mind, and most of the advice applies anywhere. The international trip list covers travel abroad.

No prices are printed on this site. Fares, rates, fees and baggage rules change and differ by operator: take them from the official source for your own dates. Lists and guides are general suggestions, written in November 2026; health notes are not medical advice.
