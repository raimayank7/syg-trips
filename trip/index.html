Trip planner: plan the days, the budget and the checklist

Build a day-by-day plan, see what it costs, print it or send it to the family. Free, no login, and your trip stays in your own browser.

Start from a sample

How it works

  1. Give the trip a name, a first day and the number of people.
  2. For each day add items: a time if you have one, what it is, what it costs, and a note such as a booking number. Items with a time sort themselves.
  3. Watch the day totals and the budget by kind of spending at the bottom.
  4. Tick off the checklist before you go.
  5. Print the plan, copy it as text, send it on WhatsApp, or copy a share link: the whole trip is inside the link, so nothing is uploaded.

Your trip is saved in this browser as you type. If you clear your browser data or switch phones, it is gone, so send yourself the share link or the text. For splitting costs among a group, use the cost splitter on the main trips site; for packing, the packing lists.

No prices on this site. The samples are empty frames: you type your own costs. Check timings, opening hours, permits and prices on official sources for your dates.
