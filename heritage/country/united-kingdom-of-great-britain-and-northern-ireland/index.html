World Heritage sites in United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland

United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland has 35 sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List: 29 cultural, 5 natural and 1 mixed. The first was inscribed in 1986 and the latest in 2024. None is currently on the List of World Heritage in Danger.
