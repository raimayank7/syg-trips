World Heritage sites in Republic of Korea

Republic of Korea has 17 sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List: 15 cultural, 2 natural and 0 mixed. The first was inscribed in 1995 and the latest in 2025. None is currently on the List of World Heritage in Danger.
