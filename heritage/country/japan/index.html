World Heritage sites in Japan

Japan has 27 sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List: 22 cultural, 5 natural and 0 mixed. The first was inscribed in 1993 and the latest in 2026. None is currently on the List of World Heritage in Danger. For Indian passport holders the Ministry of External Affairs lists Japan under e-visa (page updated 27 August 2026).
