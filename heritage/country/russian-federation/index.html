World Heritage sites in Russian Federation

Russian Federation has 33 sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List: 22 cultural, 11 natural and 0 mixed. The first was inscribed in 1990 and the latest in 2025. None is currently on the List of World Heritage in Danger.
