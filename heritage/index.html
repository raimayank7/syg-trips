    What this site gives you

    The World Heritage List names places of outstanding value to humanity, chosen by UNESCO's World Heritage Committee. Every site page here shows the facts from UNESCO's own list: the country, category, year, size, the criteria it meets and whether it is on the danger list. It then adds a plain-words weather guide taken from the nearest city in our climate data, and, where it applies, the visa facility the Indian government lists for the country and the plug type you will need.

    We do not copy UNESCO's descriptions and we do not state ticket prices or opening times, because those change. Use the official site of each place for those.

    Check before you travel. Facts come from the UNESCO World Heritage List as downloaded on 14 November 2026; climate averages are NASA POWER data for the nearest city, not for the site itself. Opening times, tickets, closures and entry rules change: use the site's own official page and your government's travel advice.
