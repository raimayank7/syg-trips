Arles, Roman and Romanesque Monuments

Arles, Roman and Romanesque Monuments is a World Heritage site in France, inscribed in 1981. UNESCO lists it as a cultural site, valued for what people built or created. UNESCO gives its size as about 65 hectares (0.7 square kilometres).

Why it is on the list

A site is added to the World Heritage List when it meets at least one of ten criteria. Arles, Roman and Romanesque Monuments meets criteria ii and iv: (ii) an important exchange of human values, ideas or skills over time; (iv) an outstanding example of a type of building, architecture or landscape.

These are our own plain-words summaries of UNESCO's criteria numbers. The full wording and UNESCO's own description of this site are on its website.
