Pitons Management Area

Pitons Management Area is a World Heritage site in Saint Lucia, inscribed in 2004. UNESCO lists it as a natural site, valued for its landscape, geology or wildlife. UNESCO gives its size as about 2,909 hectares (29.1 square kilometres).

Why it is on the list

A site is added to the World Heritage List when it meets at least one of ten criteria. Pitons Management Area meets criteria vii and viii: (vii) superlative natural phenomena or areas of exceptional natural beauty; (viii) an outstanding record of the Earth's history, life or landforms.

These are our own plain-words summaries of UNESCO's criteria numbers. The full wording and UNESCO's own description of this site are on its website.

When to visit

The nearest city in our weather data is Castries, about 22 km away. Its climate is hot and wet: the yearly average is 27.5 degrees C with about 1,443 mm of rain. By our weather rules the most comfortable months for sightseeing there are December to May. Wettest month: October (about 189 mm). Hottest month: September (highs near 29.2 degrees C). Coldest: February (nights near 25.5 degrees C).

Height, coast and local weather can make a site very different from the nearest city: use this as a guide, not a forecast.

Questions

When is the best time to visit Pitons Management Area?

Using the climate of the nearest city, Castries, the most comfortable months are December to May. This is a weather guide only; crowds, closures and festivals also matter.

Is Pitons Management Area a UNESCO World Heritage site?

Yes. It is on the UNESCO World Heritage List, inscribed in 2004 as a natural site.

Where is Pitons Management Area?

It is in Saint Lucia at about 13.81 degrees north, 61.07 degrees west.
