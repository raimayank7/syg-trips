Chengjiang Fossil Site

Chengjiang Fossil Site is a World Heritage site in China, inscribed in 2012. UNESCO lists it as a natural site, valued for its landscape, geology or wildlife. UNESCO gives its size as about 512 hectares (5.1 square kilometres).

Why it is on the list

A site is added to the World Heritage List when it meets at least one of ten criteria. Chengjiang Fossil Site meets criterion viii: (viii) an outstanding record of the Earth's history, life or landforms.

These are our own plain-words summaries of UNESCO's criteria numbers. The full wording and UNESCO's own description of this site are on its website.

When to visit

The nearest city in our weather data is Kunming, about 49 km away. Its climate is mild and moderately wet: the yearly average is 15.3 degrees C with about 828 mm of rain. By our weather rules the most comfortable months for sightseeing there are March to May and September to October. Wettest month: July (about 177 mm). Hottest month: June (highs near 26.3 degrees C). Coldest: January (nights near 1.0 degrees C).

Height, coast and local weather can make a site very different from the nearest city: use this as a guide, not a forecast.

Questions

When is the best time to visit Chengjiang Fossil Site?

Using the climate of the nearest city, Kunming, the most comfortable months are March to May and September to October. This is a weather guide only; crowds, closures and festivals also matter.

Is Chengjiang Fossil Site a UNESCO World Heritage site?

Yes. It is on the UNESCO World Heritage List, inscribed in 2012 as a natural site.

Where is Chengjiang Fossil Site?

It is in China at about 24.67 degrees north, 102.98 degrees east.
