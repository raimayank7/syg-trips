Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna

Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna is a World Heritage site in Libya, inscribed in 1982. UNESCO lists it as a cultural site, valued for what people built or created. UNESCO gives its size as about 387 hectares (3.9 square kilometres).

Why it is on the list

A site is added to the World Heritage List when it meets at least one of ten criteria. Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna meets criteria i, ii and iii: (i) a masterpiece of human creative genius; (ii) an important exchange of human values, ideas or skills over time; (iii) an exceptional testimony to a cultural tradition or a vanished civilisation.

These are our own plain-words summaries of UNESCO's criteria numbers. The full wording and UNESCO's own description of this site are on its website.

On the List of World Heritage in Danger

UNESCO currently places Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna on the List of World Heritage in Danger (record: Y 2016). That means the site faces serious threats, such as conflict, development, neglect or disaster. It can affect what visitors may see or do, so check the situation and your government's travel advice before planning a visit.

When to visit

The nearest city in our weather data is Misratah, about 80 km away. Its climate is warm and dry: the yearly average is 20.9 degrees C with about 272 mm of rain. By our weather rules the most comfortable months for sightseeing there are March to December. Wettest month: December (about 55 mm). Hottest month: August (highs near 30.2 degrees C). Coldest: February (nights near 12.2 degrees C).

Height, coast and local weather can make a site very different from the nearest city: use this as a guide, not a forecast.

Questions

When is the best time to visit Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna?

Using the climate of the nearest city, Misratah, the most comfortable months are March to December. This is a weather guide only; crowds, closures and festivals also matter.

Is Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna a UNESCO World Heritage site?

Yes. It is on the UNESCO World Heritage List, inscribed in 1982 as a cultural site.

Where is Archaeological Site of Leptis Magna?

It is in Libya at about 32.64 degrees north, 14.29 degrees east.
