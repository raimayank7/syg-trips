Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries - Wolong, Mt Siguniang and Jiajin Mountains

Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries - Wolong, Mt Siguniang and Jiajin Mountains is a World Heritage site in China, inscribed in 2006. UNESCO lists it as a natural site, valued for its landscape, geology or wildlife. UNESCO gives its size as about 924,500 hectares (9,245.0 square kilometres).

Why it is on the list

A site is added to the World Heritage List when it meets at least one of ten criteria. Sichuan Giant Panda Sanctuaries - Wolong, Mt Siguniang and Jiajin Mountains meets criterion x: (x) an important natural habitat for the conservation of biodiversity.

These are our own plain-words summaries of UNESCO's criteria numbers. The full wording and UNESCO's own description of this site are on its website.
