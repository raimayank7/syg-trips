World Heritage in Danger

58 sites in the UNESCO list we downloaded on 14 November 2026 are marked as on the List of World Heritage in Danger.

Being on the danger list means a site faces serious and specific threats, such as armed conflict, unplanned development, looting, pollution or the effects of disaster. It is a call for help and for action, not a closure: some listed sites can be visited and some cannot. Check the current situation and your government's travel advice before you plan. The list changes when UNESCO's World Heritage Committee meets.
