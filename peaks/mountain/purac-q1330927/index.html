Puracé

Puracé is 4,756 metres (15,604 feet) above sea level, which makes it the 212th highest mountain in our list of 1,127. It stands in Colombia and is the 8th highest of the 10 Colombia mountains we list there. It belongs to the Cordillera Central.

Rough temperature at the top

The nearest city in our weather data is Popayan, about 28 km away. The weather grid cell behind its figures averages about 1,679 metres above sea level. Its average daily high in August is about 24.8 degrees C. Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C for every 1,000 metres you climb, so the summit would be about 20.0 degrees C colder on average, giving a rough 4.8 degrees C in August and 4.2 degrees C in July (daily highs). This is a textbook estimate for a calm day, not a forecast: wind, cloud and the season can make it far colder, and the summit of a high mountain can be well below freezing all year.
