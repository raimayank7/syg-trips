Kasprowy Wierch

Kasprowy Wierch is 1,987 metres (6,519 feet) above sea level, which makes it the 721st highest mountain in our list of 1,127. It stands in Poland and Slovakia and is the 2nd highest of the 8 Poland mountains we list there. It belongs to the Western Tatras.

Rough temperature at the top

The nearest city in our weather data is Krakow, about 92 km away. The weather grid cell behind its figures averages about 307 metres above sea level. Its average daily high in July is about 25.3 degrees C. Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C for every 1,000 metres you climb, so the summit would be about 10.9 degrees C colder on average, giving a rough 14.4 degrees C in July and -11.0 degrees C in January (daily highs). This is a textbook estimate for a calm day, not a forecast: wind, cloud and the season can make it far colder, and the summit of a high mountain can be well below freezing all year.
