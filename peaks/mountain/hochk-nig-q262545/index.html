Hochkönig

Hochkönig is 2,941 metres (9,649 feet) above sea level, which makes it the 508th highest mountain in our list of 1,127. It stands in Austria and is the 15th highest of the 27 Austria mountains we list there. It belongs to the Hochkönig mountain range. Its topographic prominence, the height it rises above the lowest pass that links it to a higher peak, is recorded as about 2,181 metres.

Rough temperature at the top

The nearest city in our weather data is Munich, about 137 km away. The weather grid cell behind its figures averages about 616 metres above sea level. Its average daily high in July is about 23.2 degrees C. Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C for every 1,000 metres you climb, so the summit would be about 15.1 degrees C colder on average, giving a rough 8.1 degrees C in July and -13.4 degrees C in January (daily highs). This is a textbook estimate for a calm day, not a forecast: wind, cloud and the season can make it far colder, and the summit of a high mountain can be well below freezing all year.
