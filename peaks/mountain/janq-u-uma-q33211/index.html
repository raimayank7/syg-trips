Janq'u Uma

Janq'u Uma is 6,425 metres (21,079 feet) above sea level, which makes it the 104th highest mountain in our list of 1,127. It stands in Bolivia and is the 4th highest of the 19 Bolivia mountains we list there. It belongs to the Cordillera Real. Its topographic prominence, the height it rises above the lowest pass that links it to a higher peak, is recorded as about 1,957 metres.

Rough temperature at the top

The nearest city in our weather data is La Paz, about 83 km away. The weather grid cell behind its figures averages about 3,873 metres above sea level. Its average daily high in November is about 15.8 degrees C. Air cools by roughly 6.5 degrees C for every 1,000 metres you climb, so the summit would be about 16.6 degrees C colder on average, giving a rough -0.8 degrees C in November and -3.8 degrees C in July (daily highs). This is a textbook estimate for a calm day, not a forecast: wind, cloud and the season can make it far colder, and the summit of a high mountain can be well below freezing all year.
