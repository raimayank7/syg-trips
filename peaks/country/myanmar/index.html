Mountains, lakes and rivers of Myanmar

Myanmar has 4 mountains, 2 lakes and 5 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Hkakabo Razi at 5,881 metres. Its largest listed lake is Indawgyi Lake at 120 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Mekong River at about 4,350 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Myanmar in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Hkakabo Razi, stands 5,881 metres (19,295 feet) above sea level and ranks 137 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 4 mountains listed have a median height of 3,070 metres. The 2 lakes listed cover 240 square kilometres in all (93 square miles), and Indawgyi Lake ranks 442 of 568 in the world list. The 5 rivers listed add up to about 11,102 kilometres (6,898 miles), and the longest, Mekong River, is 4,350 kilometres, rank 7 of 776.

3 of the 11 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mowdok tiang (Bangladesh, Myanmar), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Myanmar.

The mountains of Myanmar one by one

  • Hkakabo Razi: 5,881 m (19,295 ft), world rank 137 of 1127. It belongs to the Himalayas. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 904 m. It lies at 28.33° north, 97.47° east.
  • Nat Ma Taung: 3,070 m (10,072 ft), world rank 481 of 1127. It belongs to the Chin Hills. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,148 m. It lies at 21.23° north, 93.90° east.
  • Popa Hill: 1,518 m (4,980 ft), world rank 838 of 1127. It belongs to the Pegu Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,150 m. It lies at 20.92° north, 95.25° east.
  • Mowdok tiang: 1,054 m (3,458 ft), world rank 938 of 1127. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 50 m. It lies at 21.79° north, 92.61° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 4 mountains listed for Myanmar differ by 4,827 m. 1 of them are on the 5,000 m line or above.

The lakes of Myanmar one by one

  • Indawgyi Lake: about 120 km² (46 square miles), world rank 442 of 568.
  • Inle Lake: about 120 km² (46 square miles), world rank 443 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 4 m.

The rivers of Myanmar one by one

  • Mekong River: about 4,350 km (2,703 miles), world rank 7 of 776. It ends at South China Sea. It is shared with Cambodia, Laos, People's Republic of China, Thailand, Vietnam, so only part of this length lies in Myanmar.
  • Salween River: about 2,815 km (1,749 miles), world rank 27 of 776. It ends at Andaman Sea. It is shared with People's Republic of China, Thailand, so only part of this length lies in Myanmar.
  • Irrawaddy River: about 2,170 km (1,348 miles), world rank 52 of 776. It ends at Andaman Sea.
  • Chindwin River: about 1,207 km (750 miles), world rank 140 of 776. It ends at Irrawaddy River.
  • Sittaung River: about 560 km (348 miles), world rank 367 of 776. It ends at Andaman Sea.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
