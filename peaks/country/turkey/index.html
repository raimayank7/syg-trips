Turkey in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Ararat, stands 5,137 metres (16,854 feet) above sea level and ranks 185 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 14 mountains listed have a median height of 3,268 metres. The 15 lakes listed cover 8,807 square kilometres in all (3,401 square miles), and Lake Van ranks 65 of 568 in the world list. The 18 rivers listed add up to about 14,961 kilometres (9,296 miles), and the longest, Euphrates, is 2,800 kilometres, rank 29 of 776.

11 of the 47 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Akhurian Reservoir (Armenia, Turkey), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Turkey.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Mount Ararat, ranks 25 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Lake Van, ranks 36 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Euphrates, ranks 43 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists.
