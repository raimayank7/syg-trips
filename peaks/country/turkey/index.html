Mountains, lakes and rivers of Turkey

Turkey has 14 mountains, 15 lakes and 18 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Ararat at 5,137 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Van at 3,755 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Euphrates at about 2,800 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
