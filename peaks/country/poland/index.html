Mountains, lakes and rivers of Poland

Poland has 8 mountains, 5 lakes and 24 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Rysy at 2,501 metres. Its largest listed lake is Szczecin Lagoon at 903 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Vistula at about 1,047 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
