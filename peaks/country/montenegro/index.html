Mountains, lakes and rivers of Montenegro

Montenegro has 3 mountains, 1 lake and 2 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Bobotov Kuk at 2,522 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Skadar at 530 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Ibar at about 276 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Mountains

  • Bobotov Kuk 2,522 m, world rank 596
  • Maglić 2,386 m, world rank 634
  • Lovćen 1,749 m, world rank 778

Lakes

  • Lake Skadar 530 km², world rank 240

Rivers

  • Ibar 276 km, world rank 605
  • Lim 220 km, world rank 669

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
