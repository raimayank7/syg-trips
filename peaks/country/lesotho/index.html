The mountains of Lesotho one by one

  • Thabana Ntlenyana: 3,482 m (11,424 ft), world rank 406 of 1127. It belongs to the Maloti Mountains. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,390 m. It lies at 29.47° south, 29.27° east.
  • Mafadi: 3,450 m (11,319 ft), world rank 412 of 1127. It belongs to the Drakensberg, Maloti Mountains. It lies at 29.20° south, 29.36° east.
  • Njesuthi: 3,410 m (11,188 ft), world rank 419 of 1127. It belongs to the Drakensberg. It lies at 29.20° south, 29.37° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 3 mountains listed for Lesotho differ by 72 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The rivers of Lesotho one by one

  • Orange River: about 2,200 km (1,367 miles), world rank 49 of 776. It ends at Atlantic Ocean. It is shared with Namibia, South Africa, so only part of this length lies in Lesotho.
  • Caledon River: about 480 km (298 miles), world rank 421 of 776. It ends at Orange River. It is shared with South Africa, so only part of this length lies in Lesotho.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
