Mountains, lakes and rivers of Democratic Republic of the Congo

Democratic Republic of the Congo has 6 mountains, 10 lakes and 17 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Karisimbi at 4,507 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Tanganyika at 32,900 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Congo River at about 4,700 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
