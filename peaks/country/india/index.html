Mountains, lakes and rivers of India

India has 15 mountains, 7 lakes and 31 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Kanchenjunga at 8,586 metres. Its largest listed lake is Vembanad at 2,033 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Indus River at about 3,180 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
