Mountains, lakes and rivers of Madagascar

Madagascar has 1 mountain, 4 lakes and 2 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Maromokotro at 2,876 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Alaotra at 900 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Betsiboka River at about 600 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Madagascar in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Maromokotro, stands 2,876 metres (9,436 feet) above sea level and ranks 524 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 4 lakes listed cover 1,596 square kilometres in all (616 square miles), and Lake Alaotra ranks 182 of 568 in the world list. The 2 rivers listed add up to about 1,164 kilometres (723 miles), and the longest, Betsiboka River, is 600 kilometres, rank 345 of 776.

None of the listed items is shared with another country in our data, so their figures lie wholly within Madagascar.

The mountains of Madagascar one by one

  • Maromokotro: 2,876 m (9,436 ft), world rank 524 of 1127. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,876 m. It lies at 14.02° south, 48.97° east.

The lakes of Madagascar one by one

  • Lake Tsimanampetsotsa: about 456 km² (176 square miles), world rank 257 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 2 m.
  • Lake Kinkony: about 140 km² (54 square miles), world rank 424 of 568.
  • Lake Ihotry: about 100 km² (39 square miles), world rank 470 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 4 m.

The rivers of Madagascar one by one

  • Mangoky River: about 564 km (350 miles), world rank 357 of 776. It ends at Mozambique Channel.
