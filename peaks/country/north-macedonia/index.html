Mountains, lakes and rivers of North Macedonia

North Macedonia has 12 mountains, 2 lakes and 3 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Korab at 2,764 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Ohrid at 349 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Vardar at about 388 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
