Mountains, lakes and rivers of Japan

Japan has 42 mountains, 10 lakes and 3 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Fuji at 3,777 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Biwa at 669 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Shinano River at about 367 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
