The mountains of North Korea one by one

  • Baekdu Mountain: 2,744 m (9,003 ft), world rank 548 of 1127. It belongs to the Changbai Mountains. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,593 m. It lies at 40.94° north, 126.83° east.
  • Mount Kumgang: 1,638 m (5,374 ft), world rank 811 of 1127. It belongs to the Taebaek Mountains. It lies at 38.66° north, 128.10° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 2 mountains listed for North Korea differ by 1,106 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The rivers of North Korea one by one

  • Yalu River: about 813 km (505 miles), world rank 253 of 776. It ends at Korea Bay. It is shared with People's Republic of China, so only part of this length lies in North Korea.
  • Tumen River: about 549 km (341 miles), world rank 375 of 776. It ends at Sea of Japan. It is shared with People's Republic of China, Russia, so only part of this length lies in North Korea.
  • Han River: about 514 km (319 miles), world rank 396 of 776. It ends at Yellow Sea. It is shared with South Korea, so only part of this length lies in North Korea.
  • Taedong River: about 439 km (273 miles), world rank 459 of 776. It ends at Korea Bay.
  • Imjin River: about 274 km (170 miles), world rank 608 of 776. It ends at Han River. It is shared with South Korea, so only part of this length lies in North Korea.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
