Mountains, lakes and rivers of Peru

Peru has 21 mountains, 2 lakes and 12 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Huascarán at 6,768 metres. Its largest listed lake is Titicaca at 8,562 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Amazon River at about 6,800 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
