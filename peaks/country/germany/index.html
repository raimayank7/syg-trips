Germany in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Zugspitze, stands 2,962 metres (9,718 feet) above sea level and ranks 503 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 12 mountains listed have a median height of 1,141 metres. The 8 lakes listed cover 1,909 square kilometres in all (737 square miles), and Szczecin Lagoon ranks 181 of 568 in the world list. The 37 rivers listed add up to about 15,190 kilometres (9,438 miles), and the longest, Danube, is 2,850 kilometres, rank 24 of 776.

21 of the 57 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Zugspitze (Austria, Germany), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Germany.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Zugspitze, ranks 57 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Szczecin Lagoon, ranks 56 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Danube, ranks 34 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists.
