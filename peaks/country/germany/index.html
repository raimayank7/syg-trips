Mountains, lakes and rivers of Germany

Germany has 12 mountains, 8 lakes and 37 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Zugspitze at 2,962 metres. Its largest listed lake is Szczecin Lagoon at 903 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Danube at about 2,850 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
