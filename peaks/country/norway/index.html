Mountains, lakes and rivers of Norway

Norway has 9 mountains, 16 lakes and 6 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Galdhøpiggen at 2,469 metres. Its largest listed lake is Mjøsa at 369 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Glomma at about 604 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
