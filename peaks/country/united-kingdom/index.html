Mountains, lakes and rivers of United Kingdom

United Kingdom has 18 mountains, 4 lakes and 11 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Queen Mary's Peak at 2,062 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lough Neagh at 392 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is River Severn at about 354 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
