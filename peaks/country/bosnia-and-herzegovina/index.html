Mountains, lakes and rivers of Bosnia and Herzegovina

Bosnia and Herzegovina has 6 mountains, 1 lake and 7 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Maglić at 2,386 metres. Its largest listed lake is Buško Blato at 56 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Sava at about 945 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
