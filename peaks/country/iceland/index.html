Mountains, lakes and rivers of Iceland

Iceland has 24 mountains, 4 lakes and 3 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Öræfajökull at 2,110 metres. Its largest listed lake is Þórisvatn at 88 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Þjórsá at about 230 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
