Azerbaijan in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Bazardüzü, stands 4,466 metres (14,652 feet) above sea level and ranks 236 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 4 mountains listed have a median height of 4,243 metres. The 4 lakes listed cover 392,071 square kilometres in all (151,379 square miles), and Caspian Sea ranks 1 of 568 in the world list. The 6 rivers listed add up to about 3,482 kilometres (2,164 miles), and the longest, Kura River, is 1,360 kilometres, rank 118 of 776.

10 of the 14 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mount Bazardüzü (Azerbaijan, Russia), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Azerbaijan.

The mountains of Azerbaijan one by one

  • Mount Bazardüzü: 4,466 m (14,652 ft), world rank 236 of 1127. It belongs to the Greater Caucasus. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,454 m. It lies at 41.23° north, 47.84° east.
  • Mount Shahdagh: 4,243 m (13,921 ft), world rank 266 of 1127. It belongs to the Greater Caucasus. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,097 m. It lies at 41.27° north, 48.00° east.
  • Mount Kaputjugh: 3,904 m (12,808 ft), world rank 342 of 1127. It belongs to the Zangezur Mountains. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,815 m. The sources disagree: values from 3,856 to 3,904 m are on record. It lies at 39.15° north, 46.00° east.
  • Porak: 2,800 m (9,186 ft), world rank 537 of 1127. It belongs to the Vardenis Mountains. It lies at 40.02° north, 45.78° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 4 mountains listed for Azerbaijan differ by 1,666 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The lakes of Azerbaijan one by one

  • Caspian Sea: about 386,400 km² (149,190 square miles), world rank 1 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 1,025 m. It is shared with Iran, Kazakhstan, Russia, Turkmenistan.
  • Lake Ağgöl: about 5,000 km² (1,931 square miles), world rank 47 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 2 m.
  • Mingachevir reservoir: about 605 km² (234 square miles), world rank 217 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 75 m.
  • Lake Sarysu: about 66 km² (25 square miles), world rank 522 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 6 m.

The rivers of Azerbaijan one by one

  • Kura River: about 1,360 km (845 miles), world rank 118 of 776. It ends at Caspian Sea. It is shared with Georgia, Turkey, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.
  • Aras River: about 1,072 km (666 miles), world rank 180 of 776. It ends at Kura River. It is shared with Armenia, Iran, Turkey, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.
  • Alazani: about 351 km (218 miles), world rank 527 of 776. It ends at Kura River, Mingachevir reservoir. It is shared with Georgia, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.
  • Iori: about 320 km (199 miles), world rank 552 of 776. It ends at Kura River. It is shared with Georgia, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.
  • Khrami River: about 201 km (125 miles), world rank 700 of 776. It ends at Kura River. It is shared with Georgia, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.
  • Vorotan River: about 178 km (111 miles), world rank 734 of 776. It ends at Aras River, Hakari River. It is shared with Armenia, so only part of this length lies in Azerbaijan.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
