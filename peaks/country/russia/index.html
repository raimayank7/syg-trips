Mountains, lakes and rivers of Russia

Russia has 49 mountains, 81 lakes and 163 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Elbrus at 5,642 metres. Its largest listed lake is Caspian Sea at 386,400 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Lena River at about 4,294 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
