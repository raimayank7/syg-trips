Albania in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Korab, stands 2,764 metres (9,068 feet) above sea level and ranks 543 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 10 mountains listed have a median height of 2,415 metres. The 4 lakes listed cover 1,225 square kilometres in all (473 square miles), and Lake Skadar ranks 240 of 568 in the world list. The 8 rivers listed add up to about 1,670 kilometres (1,038 miles), and the longest, Drin, is 280 kilometres, rank 599 of 776.

13 of the 22 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Korab (Albania, North Macedonia), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Albania.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Korab, ranks 64 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Lake Skadar, ranks 69 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Drin, ranks 115 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists. The comparison uses each country's best item, whole or shared, so a feature that two countries share counts for both.
