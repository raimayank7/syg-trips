The mountains of South Sudan one by one

  • Kinyeti: 3,187 m (10,456 ft), world rank 464 of 1127. It belongs to the Imatong Mountains. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,120 m. It lies at 3.95° north, 32.91° east.

The lakes of South Sudan one by one

  • Lake No: about 100 km² (39 square miles), world rank 471 of 568.

The rivers of South Sudan one by one

  • Nile: about 6,650 km (4,132 miles), world rank 2 of 776. It ends at Mediterranean Sea. It is shared with Burundi, Egypt, Ethiopia, Rwanda, Sudan, Uganda, so only part of this length lies in South Sudan.
  • White Nile: about 2,620 km (1,628 miles), world rank 34 of 776. It ends at Nile. It is shared with Democratic Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda, so only part of this length lies in South Sudan.
  • Mbomou River: about 800 km (497 miles), world rank 262 of 776. It ends at Ubangi River. It is shared with Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo, so only part of this length lies in South Sudan.
  • Bahr el Ghazal River: about 716 km (445 miles), world rank 297 of 776. It ends at White Nile.
  • Sobat River: about 306 km (190 miles), world rank 564 of 776. It ends at White Nile.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
