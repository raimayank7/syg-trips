Switzerland in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Dufourspitze, stands 4,634 metres (15,203 feet) above sea level and ranks 219 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 102 mountains listed have a median height of 3,921 metres. The 7 lakes listed cover 1,821 square kilometres in all (703 square miles), and Lake Geneva ranks 226 of 568 in the world list. The 7 rivers listed add up to about 3,717 kilometres (2,309 miles), and the longest, Rhine, is 1,233 kilometres, rank 136 of 776.

34 of the 116 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Zumsteinspitze (Italy, Switzerland), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Switzerland.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Dufourspitze, ranks 31 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Lake Geneva, ranks 64 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Rhine, ranks 72 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists.
