Mountains, lakes and rivers of Greece

Greece has 25 mountains, 5 lakes and 9 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Olympus at 2,918 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Prespa at 273 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Maritsa at about 490 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
