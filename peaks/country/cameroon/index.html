Mountains, lakes and rivers of Cameroon

Cameroon has 3 mountains, 2 lakes and 5 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Cameroon at 4,095 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Chad at 1,540 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Chari River at about 1,400 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Cameroon in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Cameroon, stands 4,095 metres (13,435 feet) above sea level and ranks 294 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 3 mountains listed have a median height of 2,419 metres. The 2 lakes listed cover 2,126 square kilometres in all (821 square miles), and Lake Chad ranks 124 of 568 in the world list. The 5 rivers listed add up to about 5,044 kilometres (3,134 miles), and the longest, Chari River, is 1,400 kilometres, rank 111 of 776.

8 of the 10 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Chappal Waddi (Cameroon, Nigeria), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Cameroon.

The mountains of Cameroon one by one

  • Mount Cameroon: 4,095 m (13,435 ft), world rank 294 of 1127. It belongs to the Cameroon line. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 3,901 m. It lies at 4.22° north, 9.17° east.
  • Chappal Waddi: 2,419 m (7,936 ft), world rank 630 of 1127. It belongs to the Mambilla Plateau. It lies at 7.04° north, 11.71° east.
  • Mont Ngaoui: 1,410 m (4,626 ft), world rank 865 of 1127. It belongs to the Adamawa Plateau. It lies at 6.74° north, 14.95° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 3 mountains listed for Cameroon differ by 2,685 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The lakes of Cameroon one by one

  • Lake Chad: about 1,540 km² (595 square miles), world rank 124 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 10 m. It is shared with Chad, Niger, Nigeria.
  • Lagdo Reservoir: about 586 km² (226 square miles), world rank 225 of 568.

The rivers of Cameroon one by one

  • Chari River: about 1,400 km (870 miles), world rank 111 of 776. It ends at Lake Chad. It is shared with Central African Republic, Chad, so only part of this length lies in Cameroon.
  • Benue River: about 1,370 km (851 miles), world rank 116 of 776. It ends at Niger River. It is shared with Nigeria, so only part of this length lies in Cameroon.
  • Logone River: about 1,000 km (621 miles), world rank 203 of 776. It ends at Chari River. It is shared with Central African Republic, Chad, so only part of this length lies in Cameroon.
  • Sangha: about 785 km (488 miles), world rank 266 of 776. It ends at Congo River. It is shared with Central African Republic, Republic of the Congo, so only part of this length lies in Cameroon.
  • Cross River: about 489 km (304 miles), world rank 414 of 776. It ends at Gulf of Guinea. It is shared with Nigeria, so only part of this length lies in Cameroon.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
