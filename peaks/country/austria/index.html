Mountains, lakes and rivers of Austria

Austria has 27 mountains, 2 lakes and 13 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Grossglockner at 3,798 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Constance at 536 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Danube at about 2,850 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
