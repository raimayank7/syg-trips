Mountains, lakes and rivers of Argentina

Argentina has 27 mountains, 12 lakes and 12 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Ojos del Salado at 6,893 metres. Its largest listed lake is Mar Chiquita at 5,770 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Paraná River at about 4,880 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
