Ireland in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Carrauntoohil, stands 1,039 metres (3,407 feet) above sea level and ranks 940 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 7 mountains listed have a median height of 764 metres. The 5 lakes listed cover 545 square kilometres in all (210 square miles), and Lough Corrib ranks 391 of 568 in the world list. The 3 rivers listed add up to about 744 kilometres (462 miles), and the longest, River Shannon, is 368 kilometres, rank 512 of 776.

None of the listed items is shared with another country in our data, so their figures lie wholly within Ireland.
