Mountains, lakes and rivers of Chile

Chile has 56 mountains, 16 lakes and 2 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Ojos del Salado at 6,893 metres. Its largest listed lake is Salar de Coipasa at 2,218 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Loa River at about 440 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
