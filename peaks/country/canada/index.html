Mountains, lakes and rivers of Canada

Canada has 16 mountains, 57 lakes and 19 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Logan at 5,959 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Superior at 82,350 square kilometres. Canada in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Logan, stands 5,959 metres (19,551 feet) above sea level and ranks 131 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 16 mountains listed have a median height of 4,019 metres. The 57 lakes listed cover 365,977 square kilometres in all (141,304 square miles), and Lake Superior ranks 2 of 568 in the world list. The 19 rivers listed add up to about 26,803 kilometres (16,655 miles), and the longest, Yukon River, is 3,190 kilometres, rank 16 of 776.

15 of the 92 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mount Saint Elias (Canada, United States), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Canada.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Mount Logan, ranks 15 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Lake Superior, ranks 6 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Yukon River, ranks 28 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists. The comparison uses each country's best item, whole or shared, so a feature that two countries share counts for both.
