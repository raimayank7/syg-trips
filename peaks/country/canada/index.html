Mountains, lakes and rivers of Canada

Canada has 16 mountains, 57 lakes and 19 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Logan at 5,959 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Superior at 82,350 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Yukon River at about 3,190 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
