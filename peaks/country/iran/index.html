Iran in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Damavand, stands 5,628 metres (18,465 feet) above sea level and ranks 156 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 7 mountains listed have a median height of 3,941 metres. The 5 lakes listed cover 397,150 square kilometres in all (153,340 square miles), and Caspian Sea ranks 1 of 568 in the world list. The 11 rivers listed add up to about 7,818 kilometres (4,858 miles), and the longest, Hari River, is 1,150 kilometres, rank 149 of 776.

8 of the 23 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Caspian Sea (Azerbaijan, Iran, Kazakhstan), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Iran.
