Mountains, lakes and rivers of Bolivia

Bolivia has 19 mountains, 8 lakes and 8 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Nevado Sajama at 6,542 metres. Its largest listed lake is Salar de Uyuni at 10,582 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Paraguay River at about 2,621 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
