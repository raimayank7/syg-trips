Angola in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Moco, stands 2,619 metres (8,593 feet) above sea level and ranks 579 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 7 rivers listed add up to about 13,245 kilometres (8,230 miles), and the longest, Congo River, is 4,700 kilometres, rank 6 of 776.

7 of the 8 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Congo River (Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Angola.

The mountains of Angola one by one

  • Mount Moco: 2,619 m (8,593 ft), world rank 579 of 1127. It belongs to the Bié Plateau, Q21413603. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,510 m. It lies at 12.47° south, 15.18° east.

The rivers of Angola one by one

  • Congo River: about 4,700 km (2,920 miles), world rank 6 of 776. It ends at Atlantic Ocean. It is shared with Democratic Republic of the Congo, Republic of the Congo, Zambia, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Kasai River: about 2,272 km (1,412 miles), world rank 45 of 776. It ends at Congo River. It is shared with Democratic Republic of the Congo, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Kwango River: about 1,770 km (1,100 miles), world rank 73 of 776. It ends at Kasai River. It is shared with Democratic Republic of the Congo, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Okavango River: about 1,600 km (994 miles), world rank 85 of 776. It ends at Okavango Delta. It is shared with Botswana, Namibia, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Cunene River: about 1,207 km (750 miles), world rank 141 of 776. It ends at Atlantic Ocean. It is shared with Namibia, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Kwilu River: about 965 km (600 miles), world rank 211 of 776. It ends at Kasai River. It is shared with Democratic Republic of the Congo, so only part of this length lies in Angola.
  • Cuando River: about 731 km (454 miles), world rank 284 of 776. It ends at Zambezi River. It is shared with Botswana, Namibia, Zambia, so only part of this length lies in Angola.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
