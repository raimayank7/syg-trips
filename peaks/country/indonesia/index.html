Mountains, lakes and rivers of Indonesia

Indonesia has 43 mountains, 7 lakes and 6 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Puncak Jaya at 4,884 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Toba at 1,145 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Kapuas River at about 1,143 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
