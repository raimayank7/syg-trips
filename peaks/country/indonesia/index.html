Indonesia in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Puncak Jaya, stands 4,884 metres (16,024 feet) above sea level and ranks 198 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 43 mountains listed have a median height of 2,769 metres. The 7 lakes listed cover 2,505 square kilometres in all (967 square miles), and Lake Toba ranks 148 of 568 in the world list. The 6 rivers listed add up to about 5,707 kilometres (3,546 miles), and the longest, Kapuas River, is 1,143 kilometres, rank 154 of 776.

2 of the 56 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Sepik (Indonesia, Papua New Guinea), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Indonesia.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.
