Italy in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mont Blanc, stands 4,808 metres (15,774 feet) above sea level and ranks 205 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 50 mountains listed have a median height of 3,738 metres. The 11 lakes listed cover 1,913 square kilometres in all (738 square miles), and Venetian Lagoon ranks 235 of 568 in the world list. The 15 rivers listed add up to about 4,582 kilometres (2,847 miles), and the longest, Drava, is 720 kilometres, rank 291 of 776.

30 of the 76 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mont Blanc (France, Italy), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Italy.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Mont Blanc, ranks 28 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Venetian Lagoon, ranks 67 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Drava, ranks 96 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists. The comparison uses each country's best item, whole or shared, so a feature that two countries share counts for both.
