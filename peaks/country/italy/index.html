Mountains, lakes and rivers of Italy

Italy has 50 mountains, 11 lakes and 15 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mont Blanc at 4,808 metres. Its largest listed lake is Venetian Lagoon at 550 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Drava at about 720 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
