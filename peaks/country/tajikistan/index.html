Mountains, lakes and rivers of Tajikistan

Tajikistan has 7 mountains, 3 lakes and 5 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Ismoil Somoni Peak at 7,495 metres. Its largest listed lake is Kayrakkum Reservoir at 520 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Amu Darya at about 2,620 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Tajikistan in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Ismoil Somoni Peak, stands 7,495 metres (24,590 feet) above sea level and ranks 46 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 7 mountains listed have a median height of 6,940 metres. The 3 lakes listed cover 980 square kilometres in all (378 square miles), and Kayrakkum Reservoir ranks 243 of 568 in the world list. The 5 rivers listed add up to about 7,154 kilometres (4,445 miles), and the longest, Amu Darya, is 2,620 kilometres, rank 33 of 776.

7 of the 15 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Lenin Peak (Kyrgyzstan, Soviet Union, Tajikistan), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Tajikistan.

  • Lenin Peak: 7,134 m (23,406 ft), world rank 73 of 1127. It belongs to the Trans-Alay Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,853 m. It lies at 39.34° north, 72.88° east.
  • Peak Ozodi: 7,105 m (23,310 ft), world rank 77 of 1127. It belongs to the Tajik National Academy of Sciences Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,650 m. It lies at 39.06° north, 72.01° east.
  • Independence Peak: 6,940 m (22,769 ft), world rank 82 of 1127. It belongs to the Yazgulem Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,402 m. It lies at 38.51° north, 72.35° east.
  • Pik Karla Marxa: 6,723 m (22,057 ft), world rank 92 of 1127. It belongs to the Shakhdara Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,693 m. It lies at 37.16° north, 72.48° east.
  • Pik Garmo: 6,595 m (21,637 ft), world rank 98 of 1127. It belongs to the Tajik National Academy of Sciences Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 1,265 m. It lies at 38.81° north, 72.07° east.
  • Khazret Sultan: 4,643 m (15,233 ft), world rank 218 of 1127. It belongs to the Hisar Range. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 564 m. It lies at 38.95° north, 68.17° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 7 mountains listed for Tajikistan differ by 2,852 m. 6 of them are on the 5,000 m line or above.

  • Syr Darya: about 2,212 km (1,374 miles), world rank 48 of 776. It ends at North Aral Sea. It is shared with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, so only part of this length lies in Tajikistan.
  • Panj River: about 921 km (572 miles), world rank 223 of 776. It ends at Amu Darya. It is shared with Afghanistan, so only part of this length lies in Tajikistan.
  • Zeravshan: about 877 km (545 miles), world rank 237 of 776. It ends at Amu Darya. It is shared with Uzbekistan, so only part of this length lies in Tajikistan.
  • Vakhsh River: about 524 km (326 miles), world rank 388 of 776. It ends at Amu Darya. It is shared with Kyrgyzstan, so only part of this length lies in Tajikistan.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
