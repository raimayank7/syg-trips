Mountains, lakes and rivers of Venezuela

Venezuela has 6 mountains, 2 lakes and 7 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Pico Bolívar at 4,978 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Maracaibo at 13,210 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Rio Negro at about 2,250 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Venezuela in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Pico Bolívar, stands 4,978 metres (16,332 feet) above sea level and ranks 195 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 6 mountains listed have a median height of 2,994 metres. The 2 lakes listed cover 13,554 square kilometres in all (5,233 square miles), and Lake Maracaibo ranks 16 of 568 in the world list. The 7 rivers listed add up to about 8,413 kilometres (5,228 miles), and the longest, Rio Negro, is 2,250 kilometres, rank 47 of 776.

6 of the 15 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Pico da Neblina (Brazil, Venezuela), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Venezuela.

The mountains of Venezuela one by one

  • Pico Bolívar: 4,978 m (16,332 ft), world rank 195 of 1127. It belongs to the Cordillera de Mérida. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 3,957 m. It lies at 8.54° north, 71.05° west.
  • Humboldt peak: 4,940 m (16,207 ft), world rank 197 of 1127. It belongs to the Cordillera de Mérida. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 440 m. It lies at 8.55° north, 71.00° west.
  • Pico da Neblina: 2,994 m (9,823 ft), world rank 499 of 1127. It belongs to the Cerro de la Neblina. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,886 m. It lies at 0.81° north, 66.01° west.
  • Mount Roraima: 2,810 m (9,219 ft), world rank 534 of 1127. It belongs to the Pakaraima Mountains. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 2,338 m. It lies at 5.14° north, 60.76° west.
  • Kukenán Tepuy: 2,680 m (8,793 ft), world rank 562 of 1127. It belongs to the Pakaraima Mountains. It lies at 5.20° north, 60.83° west.
  • Auyán Tepuy: 2,500 m (8,202 ft), world rank 606 of 1127. It lies at 5.90° north, 62.55° west.

The tallest and the lowest of the 6 mountains listed for Venezuela differ by 2,478 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The lakes of Venezuela one by one

  • Lake Maracaibo: about 13,210 km² (5,100 square miles), world rank 16 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 60 m.
  • Lake Valencia: about 344 km² (133 square miles), world rank 291 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 75 m.

The rivers of Venezuela one by one

  • Rio Negro: about 2,250 km (1,398 miles), world rank 47 of 776. It ends at Amazon River. It is shared with Brazil, Colombia, so only part of this length lies in Venezuela.
  • Orinoco River: about 2,140 km (1,330 miles), world rank 53 of 776. It ends at Atlantic Ocean. It is shared with Colombia, so only part of this length lies in Venezuela.
  • Apure River: about 1,580 km (982 miles), world rank 88 of 776. It ends at Orinoco River.
  • Essequibo River: about 1,014 km (630 miles), world rank 199 of 776. It ends at Atlantic Ocean. It is shared with Guyana, so only part of this length lies in Venezuela.
  • Caroní River: about 765 km (475 miles), world rank 276 of 776. It ends at Orinoco River.
  • Catatumbo River: about 338 km (210 miles), world rank 539 of 776. It ends at Lake Maracaibo. It is shared with Colombia, so only part of this length lies in Venezuela.
  • Casiquiare canal: about 326 km (203 miles), world rank 547 of 776. It ends at Rio Negro.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
