New Zealand in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Aoraki / Mount Cook, stands 3,724 metres (12,218 feet) above sea level and ranks 373 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 1 river listed adds up to about 425 kilometres (264 miles), and the longest, Waikato River, is 425 kilometres, rank 471 of 776.

None of the listed items is shared with another country in our data, so their figures lie wholly within New Zealand.
