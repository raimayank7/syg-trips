Mountains, lakes and rivers of Ukraine

Ukraine has 6 mountains, 11 lakes and 32 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Hoverla at 2,061 metres. Its largest listed lake is Syvash Bay at 2,560 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Dnieper at about 2,285 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
