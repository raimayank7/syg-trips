Spain in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Teide, stands 3,715 metres (12,188 feet) above sea level and ranks 374 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 29 mountains listed have a median height of 2,446 metres. The 1 lake listed covers 303 square kilometres in all (117 square miles), and Mar Menor ranks 310 of 568 in the world list. The 19 rivers listed add up to about 8,576 kilometres (5,329 miles), and the longest, Tagus, is 1,007 kilometres, rank 201 of 776.

11 of the 49 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Vignemale (France, Spain), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Spain.
