Mountains, lakes and rivers of Spain

Spain has 29 mountains, 1 lake and 19 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Teide at 3,715 metres. Its largest listed lake is Mar Menor at 303 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Tagus at about 1,007 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
