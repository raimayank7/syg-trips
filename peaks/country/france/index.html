Mountains, lakes and rivers of France

France has 37 mountains, 3 lakes and 43 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mont Blanc at 4,808 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lake Geneva at 581 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Rhine at about 1,233 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
