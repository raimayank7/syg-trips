Mountains, lakes and rivers of Israel

Israel has 8 mountains, 2 lakes and 1 river in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Meron at 1,208 metres. Its largest listed lake is Dead Sea at 1,050 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Jordan River at about 252 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.

Israel in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Meron, stands 1,208 metres (3,963 feet) above sea level and ranks 908 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 8 mountains listed have a median height of 826 metres. The 2 lakes listed cover 1,218 square kilometres in all (470 square miles), and Dead Sea ranks 165 of 568 in the world list. The 1 river listed adds up to about 252 kilometres (157 miles), and the longest, Jordan River, is 252 kilometres, rank 626 of 776.

4 of the 11 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mount Scopus (Israel, Palestine), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside Israel.

The mountains of Israel one by one

  • Meron: 1,208 m (3,963 ft), world rank 908 of 1127. It lies at 33.00° north, 35.41° east.
  • Mount Herzl: 834 m (2,736 ft), world rank 988 of 1127. It belongs to the Jerusalem mountains. It lies at 31.77° north, 35.18° east.
  • Mount Scopus: 826 m (2,710 ft), world rank 991 of 1127. It belongs to the Mount of Olives ridge. It lies at 31.79° north, 35.24° east.
  • Mount of Olives: 826 m (2,710 ft), world rank 992 of 1127. It belongs to the Mount of Olives ridge. It lies at 31.78° north, 35.25° east.
  • Har HaMenuchot: 750 m (2,461 ft), world rank 1005 of 1127. It belongs to the Jerusalem mountains. It lies at 31.80° north, 35.18° east.
  • Temple Mount: 743 m (2,438 ft), world rank 1010 of 1127. It belongs to the Jerusalem mountains. It lies at 31.78° north, 35.24° east.
  • Mount Tabor: 530 m (1,739 ft), world rank 1047 of 1127. It belongs to the Lower Galilee. It lies at 32.69° north, 35.39° east.
  • Mount Arbel: 282 m (925 ft), world rank 1094 of 1127. It belongs to the Lower Galilee. Its prominence, the climb from the lowest saddle that links it to a higher peak, is given as 380 m. It lies at 32.82° north, 35.50° east.

The tallest and the lowest of the 8 mountains listed for Israel differ by 926 m. None reaches 5,000 m.

The lakes of Israel one by one

  • Dead Sea: about 1,050 km² (405 square miles), world rank 165 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 360 m. It is shared with Jordan, Palestine.
  • Sea of Galilee: about 168 km² (65 square miles), world rank 399 of 568. Its greatest depth is recorded as 44 m.

The rivers of Israel one by one

  • Jordan River: about 252 km (157 miles), world rank 626 of 776. It ends at Dead Sea. It is shared with Jordan, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria, so only part of this length lies in Israel.

About these figures. Heights, areas and lengths come from Wikidata (CC0), downloaded on 14 November 2026. Measurements differ between sources, and rivers and lakes change; where Wikidata holds several values we use the middle one and say so. Treat the numbers as good estimates, not survey results.
