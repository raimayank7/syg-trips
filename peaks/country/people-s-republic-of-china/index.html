People's Republic of China in numbers

The highest listed mountain, Mount Everest, stands 8,848 metres (29,030 feet) above sea level and ranks 1 among the 1127 mountains in our list. The 50 mountains listed have a median height of 7,294 metres. The 28 lakes listed cover 38,693 square kilometres in all (14,940 square miles), and Poyang Lake ranks 45 of 568 in the world list. The 37 rivers listed add up to about 64,716 kilometres (40,213 miles), and the longest, Yangtze River, is 6,300 kilometres, rank 3 of 776.

38 of the 115 items are shared with at least one other country, for example Mount Everest (Nepal, People's Republic of China), so their figures describe the whole feature, not only the part inside People's Republic of China.

Heights, areas and lengths are the figures from Wikidata as downloaded on the date given below, converted here to feet and miles. Open any item for its own page with comparisons and a note on how the measurement is made.

Among the 123 countries with at least five mountains, lakes and rivers in our lists, its highest mountain, Mount Everest, ranks 1 of the 114 countries that have one in our lists; its largest lake, Poyang Lake, ranks 33 of the 96 countries that have one in our lists; its longest river, Yangtze River, ranks 11 of the 120 countries that have one in our lists. The comparison uses each country's best item, whole or shared, so a feature that two countries share counts for both.
