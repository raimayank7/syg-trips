Mountains, lakes and rivers of People's Republic of China

People's Republic of China has 50 mountains, 28 lakes and 37 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Mount Everest at 8,848 metres. Its largest listed lake is Poyang Lake at 5,100 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Yangtze River at about 6,300 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
