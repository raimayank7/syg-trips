Mountains, lakes and rivers of Brazil

Brazil has 5 mountains, 2 lakes and 26 rivers in our lists. Its highest listed mountain is Pico da Neblina at 2,994 metres. Its largest listed lake is Lagoa dos Patos at 10,000 square kilometres. Its longest listed river is Amazon River at about 6,800 kilometres. Rankings are the world rankings, so a rank of 500 is a rank among all the mountains in our list, not within the country.
