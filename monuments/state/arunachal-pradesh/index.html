Arunachal Pradesh in the national list

Arunachal Pradesh has 3 of the 3,698 entries in the list, which is 0.1% of the country total. The entries sit in 3 localities; the busiest are Bhalukpong (Balipara) (1), Debang Valley (1), Near sadia (1). 1 of the 2 districts have only one entry. By the words in their names, 1 temples.

The ASI list gives each entry a name, a locality and a district, and nothing about its condition or access. A small state can have few entries and still hold important sites, so use this count as a size of the list, not a measure of heritage. Open the district folds above to see every entry, and check the ASI or the state tourism office before you travel.

How Arunachal Pradesh compares with the other states

With 3 entries, Arunachal Pradesh is number 30 of the 33 states and union territories in the list when they are sorted from the most entries to the fewest. Sikkim has the same number of entries (3). Only 2 states have fewer. The middle state of the list has 44 entries, so Arunachal Pradesh sits far below that middle point; 9 of the 33 states have fewer than ten entries. The longest list is that of Uttar Pradesh with 743 entries, which is 248 times the size of this one.

These 3 entries are the whole list of centrally protected monuments in Arunachal Pradesh. State governments protect many more monuments under their own laws, and those are not in this list, so a short list says how much the central government protects here, not how much there is to see. For the rest, ask the state department of archaeology or the tourism office.

Check before you visit. This list is the Archaeological Survey of India's list of centrally protected monuments, as downloaded on 14 November 2026. It gives no opening times, ticket prices or access details, and it can change as monuments are added or removed. Check the ASI's own pages, the state tourism office and local sources before you go. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.
