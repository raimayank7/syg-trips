Centrally protected monuments in Gujarat

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 205 centrally protected monuments and sites in Gujarat, spread over 24 districts. The districts with the most are Ahmedabad (65), Panchmahals (42), Mehsana (13). By the names in the list, there are 48 mosques and masjids, 43 temples, 34 forts and fortifications, 31 tombs and mausoleums.
