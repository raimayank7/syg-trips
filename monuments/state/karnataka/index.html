Centrally protected monuments in Karnataka

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 506 centrally protected monuments and sites in Karnataka (administered by the ASI's Bangalore Circle, Dharwad Circle and Hampi Circle) Bangalore Circle), spread over 30 districts. The districts with the most are Bagalkot (101), Vijayapura (82), Bellary (75). By the names in the list, there are 224 temples, 55 inscriptions and rock edicts, 40 forts and fortifications, 36 mosques and masjids.
