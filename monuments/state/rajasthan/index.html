Centrally protected monuments in Rajasthan

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 163 centrally protected monuments and sites in Rajasthan (administered by the ASI's Jaipur Circle and Jodhpur Circle) Jaipur Circle), spread over 26 districts. The districts with the most are Bharatpur (22), Ajmer (22), Tonk (17). By the names in the list, there are 43 temples, 37 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 22 forts and fortifications, 14 kos minars and pillars.

It gives no opening times, ticket prices or access details, and it can change as monuments are added or removed. Check the ASI's own pages, the state tourism office and local sources before you go. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.
