Centrally protected monuments in Maharashtra

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 286 centrally protected monuments and sites in Maharashtra (administered by the ASI's Aurangabad Circle, Mumbai Circle and Nagpur Circle) Aurangabad Circle), spread over 31 districts. The districts with the most are Raigad (49), Ahmednagar (29), Chandrapur (21). By the names in the list, there are 106 temples, 55 forts and fortifications, 44 caves and rock-cut sites, 19 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths.
