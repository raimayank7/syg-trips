Centrally protected monuments in Assam

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 55 centrally protected monuments and sites in Assam (administered by the ASI's Guwahati Circle), spread over 13 districts. The districts with the most are Sivasagar (17), Biswanath (6), Dima-Hasao (N.C.Hills) (6). By the names in the list, there are 9 temples, 5 tombs and mausoleums, 5 stepwells, tanks and wells, 4 caves and rock-cut sites.
