Centrally protected monuments in Punjab

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 33 centrally protected monuments and sites in Punjab (administered by the ASI's Chandigarh Circle), spread over 9 districts. The districts with the most are Jalandhar (15), Ludhiana (6), Gurdaspur (3). By the names in the list, there are 13 kos minars and pillars, 8 forts and fortifications, 7 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 2 palaces and havelis.

Check before you visit. This list is the Archaeological Survey of India's list of centrally protected monuments, as downloaded on 14 November 2026. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.
