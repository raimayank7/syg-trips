Centrally protected monuments in Delhi

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 172 centrally protected monuments and sites in Delhi (administered by the ASI's Delhi Circle), spread over 7 districts. The districts with the most are South Delhi (74), South East Delhi (38), North Delhi (31). By the names in the list, there are 63 tombs and mausoleums, 41 forts and fortifications, 29 mosques and masjids, 13 palaces and havelis.
