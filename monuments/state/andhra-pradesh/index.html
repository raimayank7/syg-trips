Centrally protected monuments in Andhra Pradesh

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 135 centrally protected monuments and sites in Andhra Pradesh (administered by the ASI's Amaravati Circle), spread over 13 districts. The districts with the most are Anantapuramu (23), Kadapa YSR (21), Guntur (17). By the names in the list, there are 61 temples, 38 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 19 forts and fortifications, 9 caves and rock-cut sites.
