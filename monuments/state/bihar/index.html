Centrally protected monuments in Bihar

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 70 centrally protected monuments and sites in Bihar (administered by the ASI's Patna Circle), spread over 17 districts. The districts with the most are Gaya (11), Patna (11), Jehanabad (9). By the names in the list, there are 18 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 8 caves and rock-cut sites, 6 tombs and mausoleums, 5 forts and fortifications.
