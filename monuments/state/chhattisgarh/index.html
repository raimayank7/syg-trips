Centrally protected monuments in Chhattisgarh

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 46 centrally protected monuments and sites in Chhattisgarh (administered by the ASI's Raipur Circle), spread over 16 districts. The districts with the most are Janjgir Champa (10), South Bastar Dantewada (8), Bilaspur (7). By the names in the list, there are 28 temples, 5 forts and fortifications, 5 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 3 inscriptions and rock edicts.

Check before you visit. This list is the Archaeological Survey of India's list of centrally protected monuments, as downloaded on 14 November 2026. It gives no opening times, ticket prices or access details, and it can change as monuments are added or removed. Check the ASI's own pages, the state tourism office and local sources before you go. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.
