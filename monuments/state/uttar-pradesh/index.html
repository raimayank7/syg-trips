Centrally protected monuments in Uttar Pradesh

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 743 centrally protected monuments and sites in Uttar Pradesh (administered by the ASI's Agra Circle, Jhansi Circle, Lucknow Circle, Meerut Circle and Sarnath Circle) Agra Circle), spread over 65 districts. The districts with the most are Lalitpur (72), Agra (70), Lucknow (60). By the names in the list, there are 151 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 133 temples, 130 tombs and mausoleums, 80 forts and fortifications.
