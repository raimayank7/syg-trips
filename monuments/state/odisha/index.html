Centrally protected monuments in Odisha

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 81 centrally protected monuments and sites in Odisha (administered by the ASI's Bhubaneswar Circle), spread over 15 districts. The districts with the most are Khurda (30), Cuttack (10), Jajpur (10). By the names in the list, there are 49 temples, 10 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 8 forts and fortifications, 5 stepwells, tanks and wells.
