Centrally protected monuments in Madhya Pradesh

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 291 centrally protected monuments and sites in Madhya Pradesh (administered by the ASI's Bhopal Circle and Jabalpur Circle) Bhopal Circle), spread over 37 districts. The districts with the most are Dhar (66), Chhatarpur (25), Ashok Nagar (18). By the names in the list, there are 93 temples, 43 forts and fortifications, 29 palaces and havelis, 23 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths.
