Centrally protected monuments in Uttarakhand

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 44 centrally protected monuments and sites in Uttarakhand (administered by the ASI's Dehradun Circle), spread over 10 districts. The districts with the most are Almora (19), Chamoli (6), Dehradun (6). By the names in the list, there are 28 temples, 3 inscriptions and rock edicts, 2 stepwells, tanks and wells, 2 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths.

Check before you visit. This list is the Archaeological Survey of India's list of centrally protected monuments, as downloaded on 14 November 2026. It gives no opening times, ticket prices or access details, and it can change as monuments are added or removed. Check the ASI's own pages, the state tourism office and local sources before you go. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.

Source: Archaeological Survey of India, list of centrally protected monuments/sites (asi.nic.in/pdf/CPM_List.pdf), downloaded 14 November 2026. The ASI list gives the state total of 44, which matches.
