Centrally protected monuments in Tamil Nadu

The Archaeological Survey of India lists 412 centrally protected monuments and sites in Tamil Nadu (administered by the ASI's Chennai Circle and Tiruchirapalli Circle) Chennai Circle), spread over 30 districts. The districts with the most are Kanchipuram (156), Pudukkottai (109), Villupuram (21). By the names in the list, there are 161 ancient sites, mounds and megaliths, 138 temples, 48 caves and rock-cut sites, 30 forts and fortifications.
