Caves and rock-cut sites among the protected monuments

171 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as caves and rock-cut sites. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "cave", "rock cut", "rock-cut", "rock shelter" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Tamil Nadu (48), Maharashtra (44), Madhya Pradesh (19), Andhra Pradesh (9), Bihar (8).
