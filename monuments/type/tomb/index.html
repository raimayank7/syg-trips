Tombs and mausoleums among the protected monuments

342 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as tombs and mausoleums. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "tomb", "mausoleum", "maqbara", "rauza" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (130), Delhi (63), Gujarat (31), Karnataka (29), Madhya Pradesh (18).
