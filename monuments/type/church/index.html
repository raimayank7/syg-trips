Churches and colonial buildings among the protected monuments

55 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as churches and colonial buildings. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "church", "cathedral", "convent", "basilica" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (17), Goa (8), Maharashtra (7), West Bengal (7), Tamil Nadu (6).
