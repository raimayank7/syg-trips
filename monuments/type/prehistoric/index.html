Ancient sites, mounds and megaliths among the protected monuments

576 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as ancient sites, mounds and megaliths. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "ancient site", "mound", "megalith", "dolmen" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Tamil Nadu (161), Uttar Pradesh (151), Andhra Pradesh (38), Rajasthan (37), Madhya Pradesh (23).
