Mosques and masjids among the protected monuments

241 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as mosques and masjids. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "mosque", "masjid", "idgah", "dargah" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (51), Gujarat (48), Karnataka (36), Delhi (29), Madhya Pradesh (19).
