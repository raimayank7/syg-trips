Forts and fortifications among the protected monuments

425 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as forts and fortifications. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "fort", "fortification", "bastion", "rampart" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (80), Maharashtra (55), Madhya Pradesh (43), Delhi (41), Karnataka (40).
