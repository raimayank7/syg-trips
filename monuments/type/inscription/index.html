Inscriptions and rock edicts among the protected monuments

160 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as inscriptions and rock edicts. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "inscription", "edict", "epigraph" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Karnataka (55), Tamil Nadu (24), Uttar Pradesh (20), Rajasthan (11), Maharashtra (9).
