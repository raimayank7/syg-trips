Stepwells, tanks and wells among the protected monuments

131 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as stepwells, tanks and wells. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "stepwell", "step well", "baoli", "baori" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (32), Gujarat (16), Karnataka (14), Maharashtra (14), Tamil Nadu (10).
