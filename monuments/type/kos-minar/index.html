Kos Minars and pillars among the protected monuments

185 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as kos minars and pillars. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "kos minar", "kosminar", "pillar", "minar" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Uttar Pradesh (59), Haryana (50), Karnataka (14), Rajasthan (14), Punjab (13).
