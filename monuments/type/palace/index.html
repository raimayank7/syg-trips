Palaces and havelis among the protected monuments

130 of the monuments and sites in the ASI list have a name that marks them as palaces and havelis. This grouping is ours: we match words such as "palace", "haveli", "mahal", "garden" in the official names, so it is a guide, not an ASI classification, and a monument may fall in more than one group or in none. The states with the most are Madhya Pradesh (29), Uttar Pradesh (24), Karnataka (18), Delhi (13), Rajasthan (11).
