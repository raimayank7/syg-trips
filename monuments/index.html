    About this list

    These are the monuments and sites that the Government of India has declared to be of national importance, which the Archaeological Survey of India protects and maintains. The list gives the name of each, its locality and its district. It is a long list, and most entries are small: a single temple, a gateway, a stretch of wall, a mound, an inscription. Only some are visitor attractions in the ordinary sense.

    We show the official list as published, with a search, state and district pages, and a grouping by type. We do not give opening times or ticket prices, because the list does not contain them and they change; the ASI and state tourism offices are the sources for those.

    Check before you visit. This list is the Archaeological Survey of India's list of centrally protected monuments, as downloaded on 14 November 2026. It gives no opening times, ticket prices or access details, and it can change as monuments are added or removed. Check the ASI's own pages, the state tourism office and local sources before you go. Many monuments are small or remote and are not open to visitors in the usual way.
