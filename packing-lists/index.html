Make a list for your own trip

How these lists are built

Every list starts from the same core that almost any trip needs: documents and money, everyday clothes, toiletries, health items, the phone kit and a few useful extras. To that, each page adds the things special to its kind of trip, with the reasons, the mistakes people commonly make, and the questions they ask.

On each page you can tick items as you pack (the ticks stay in your browser), print the list, or send it on WhatsApp to whoever is packing with you.

Which list should I use?

Pick the one closest to your trip. If two apply, such as a monsoon trek or a road trip to the hills with a baby, use the packing list maker: choose the main kind of trip and tick the boxes for children, rain or cold, and it combines them and works out clothing counts for your number of nights.

Three rules that apply to every trip

  1. The day bag holds what you cannot lose: documents, money, medicines, phone, charger, keys, and a change of clothes. It stays with you, never in the hold or the boot.
  2. Pack for the weather and the purpose, not for every possibility. Most "just in case" items come home unused. Anything truly forgotten can usually be bought.
  3. Check your operator's bag rules. Airlines and some buses and trains limit the size, weight and contents of bags, and the rules differ. Read your own operator's page.
Can I change the lists?

The printed and copied lists are plain text, so you can add and remove lines freely. The maker also lets you combine kinds of trip.

Are the ticks saved?

Yes, in this browser on this device, separately for each list.

No prices are printed on this site. Fares, rates, fees and baggage rules change and differ by operator: take them from the official source for your own dates. Lists and guides are general suggestions, written in November 2026; health notes are not medical advice.
