Tips for this kind of trip

  • Take a break every two hours or so. Change drivers if you can, and never drive when sleepy: stop and nap.
  • Avoid driving at night on unfamiliar highways and hill roads.
  • Everyone wears a seat belt, in the back seats too. Children ride in the rear, in a child seat suited to their size.
  • Fill fuel when the tank is half empty on long or remote stretches.
  • Speed limits differ by road and state and are enforced by camera. Follow the signs.
  • Share your live location with someone at home.
  • Keep heavy luggage low and secured, not loose on the parcel shelf where it can fly forward in a sudden stop.
  • Use our fuel cost calculator to estimate the fuel bill with your own car's mileage and today's price at your pump.

Questions

Which documents must I carry while driving?

The driving licence, the vehicle's registration certificate, valid insurance and a valid PUC certificate. Digital copies in the government's DigiLocker or mParivahan apps are accepted; keep the originals or copies as a backup.

How often should I stop on a long drive?

About every two hours, for ten to fifteen minutes. Stretch, drink water and let the driver rest.
